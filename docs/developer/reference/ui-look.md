# The web UI: conventions, look and motion

How pages are built and how they look and move: amounts, signs, tiles, sheets, dark mode, the design system. Dates in headings are when a decision was made. Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-07; the rules summary is in `AGENTS.md`.

## Web UI

There is no JavaScript build step; do not add one. Templates extend `base.html`; `partials/` holds fragments returned to htmx. Mutating forms use `hx-post` (htmx 2 sends `DELETE` parameters in the URL, so use `POST` routes such as `/{id}/delete`). Handlers answer success with `htmx_redirect(location, flash=...)` and failures with a 4xx plus `HX-Retarget` pointing at an error element, via `routers/web/utils.py`; `static/app.js` lets htmx swap those 4xx responses.

UI conventions (keep pages working on phones first):
- Layout: `base.html` renders a slim top bar (the wordmark and an avatar that leads to Settings) and a bottom tab bar of four tabs (Home, Budgets, Accounts, Categories) with the "+" **docked in the middle of the bar** (`.tabbar.has-plus`, `a.fab`; nothing floats over the page, 2026-10-23) on phones (Transactions has no tab and no icon: it is reached from "View all" on Home and the sidebar); from 60rem up a fixed sidebar (new-transaction button, those links, then Transactions last (Home, Budgets, Accounts, Categories, Transactions), Settings at the bottom) replaces the top bar, tab bar and "+", and the page is one calm column. Pass `active` for the current section and `hide_fab=True` on form pages (it hides the "+" and the sidebar's button). Sign-out lives at the bottom of the profile page, which is reached from Settings. The look is set by the tokens in `tokens.css` (the passbook look: green-grey paper, blue-black ink for text, royal-blue ink for actions, green and red only for money in and out, light and dark; see "The passbook look" below) and the self-hosted Anek Latin font (`static/fonts`, `@font-face` inline in `base.html` with the hashed URL and a preload); keep text inputs at 16px or more so iOS does not zoom, and prefer our own chips and sheets to native pickers so iOS and Android look the same. The target design is the mockup the owner approved (calm, minimal, quick entry): Home with one number and the transaction rows without card borders (new transactions start from the floating "+" on phones and the sidebar button on wide screens).
- **The accounts page (2026-11-09)** (`accounts.html`, `accounts_page`, `kinds`, `kind_groups` and `mark_closed` in `routers/web/accounts.py`, the `accountsPage` block in `app.js`; the end of `style.css`): **one page, no chips, no Asset/Liability/Equity** (the double-entry type names stay out of the app; `?tab=` in an old link is ignored and lands here). A title row, then the **Net worth** hero (Assets less Liabilities, `.cat-hero`, "N accounts" and the budget key under it), then one **kind** per `<section class="acc-kind">` with **2.1rem between kinds** (2026-11-09: the headings were 13px grey and the rows ran together): a heading block (`.acc-head`: the name as an `h2`, 1.25rem bold in the text colour, and under it one quiet line, the kind's total ("X owed" for Credit Cards, Loans and other liabilities; **no minus signs** except on a negative net worth) and "N accounts", the open ones; nothing sits at the right, so the cards' own figures are the only ones on that edge) over **a row of cards that scrolls sideways** (`.acc-rail`, scroll-snap, the next card peeking in at the edge; the rail bleeds to the page edge with `margin-inline` and padding). The kinds come in this order (`KIND_ORDER`): Bank Accounts, Cash, Credit Cards, Investments, Loans, then groups the person made (`kind_groups`: a group is a built-in one, one the person made (even empty) or an account with accounts inside it; alphabetical), then **Other** (plain accounts directly under Assets) and **Other you owe** (the same under Liabilities). The five built-in kinds and any group the person made always show, empty ones too (a heading reading "0.00 · 0 accounts" and only the dashed add card); Other and Other you owe show only with plain accounts; Equity and the contact accounts are not listed. **A card** (`.acc-card`, `a` to the account's page, 13.25rem wide, all the same size) carries the name with the budget dot, the balance and **a quiet line only when there is something to say**: **Bill due 15 Nov** (red, `.due`, within `DUE_SOON_DAYS`; "Bill overdue" once past) for an account with its statement and due days and something left to pay, else **No entry since 12 Sep** when its newest entry (up to today; entries dated ahead do not count) is more than `STALE_DAYS` (30) old, else **No entries yet**; a card with a recent entry has no line ("Last entry 9 Nov" was on nearly every card and was never news), and cards stay the same size with the balance at the bottom. Both come from `AccountService.glance` in two queries however many accounts there are (`TransactionRepository.last_entry_dates`, and `posting_facts` for the accounts with bill days, shared with `bill()` through `_bill_of`; `CEILING` for `/accounts` is 5). **The last card of a row is a dashed "+ Add account"** (`.acc-card-add`, opens `/accounts/new?parent=<group>`; for "Other" `?root=assets|liabilities`); the page ends with one dashed `.add-row`, **"+ New group"** (`/accounts/new-group`, 2026-11-10; the form is `group_form.html`: a name, `.scope-seg` radios "Money I own / Money I owe" and the budget `.check-row`, nothing chosen, no balance field), and nothing else, since every kind has its own add card. **A row that runs past the screen has no scroll indicator** (a thin track under it was built and removed on 2026-11-09 at the owner's request: the next card peeking in at the edge says there is more, and the heading says how many). **Nothing folds**; **every open account is listed**, used or not; **closed** ones (a card with the `closed` badge, `.closed-hidden`) wait behind one **Load more** button (`show-closed`), and a kind whose accounts are all closed hides whole. **The form** (`account_form.html`): "Under" became **Kind**, one chip per group (`[data-kind-chip]`, `aria-pressed`), nothing chosen unless the link says (the hidden `select[name=parent_id]` still carries the value, `data-root` and `data-start` as before, so the budget switch, the bill days and the balance label "Amount you owe now" follow the kind: `budgetChoice` in `app.js`). Opened for plain accounts of a type, or inside an account ("Add sub-account"), it says "Kind · Other" or "Inside · <account>" instead of chips (`_placed`). Editing a group (an account with accounts inside) shows no kind. An account's page shows its **kind** under the name ("Bank Accounts", "Other"), never "Assets", and its back link, close and delete all go to `/accounts`. `tests/test_web_accounts_page.py`, `tests/browser/test_accounts_page.py`.
- **Closing accounts** (`AccountService.close` / `reopen`, `accounts.closed_on`; `POST /api/accounts/{account_id}/close`, `POST /accounts/{account_id}/reopen`, the Close/Reopen card on the account page): a closed account (and everything inside it) keeps its history but can't be posted to (`TransactionService._resolve`; an entry that already uses it stays editable), can't get sub-accounts or a balance change, and leaves the pickers (`posting_groups`, keeping what an entry being edited uses), the accounts list (behind "Load more"), the budget once a closed category has nothing left in the month, and the assistants' `list_accounts` (`include_closed`). Closing is refused while anything inside holds a balance (now or in a future-dated entry) or a running recurring rule uses it; Reopening reopens the account, what is inside it and the groups above it. **For a category (Expenses/Income) the word is Delete**: the menu on a category's page and the Edit page's button call `POST /accounts/{account_id}/delete`, which removes an unused category for good and, when it has entries, subcategories or is in a rule, closes it instead (`category-deleted`; it leaves the lists and pickers, stays in the months where it has amounts, and "Restore category" reopens it); a category's balance is spending, so closing one never waits for it to be empty. Accounts (Assets/Liabilities) keep Close and Reopen, in the account's menu.
- Lists use `partials/transaction_list.html` (rows from `transactions.to_row`, grouped by day with the `friendly_date` filter); format amounts with the `money` filter. Avoid tables — they overflow on phones.
- Rows are links to a detail or edit page; destructive actions live on that page, not on list rows.
- Forms that can be opened from several places accept a `back` path and return there after saving; always pass it through `safe_back`, which only allows local paths of the web UI (not `/api`, `/mcp`, `/static`…). **The bar's "+" and the sidebar's button follow the page (2026-11-01)**: an account's page (when it can be posted to and is open) and a category's page set `new_transaction_url` (`/transactions/new?account=<id>&back=/accounts/<id>`, `?category=<id>&back=/categories/<id>`), which `base.html` uses for both; every other page keeps `/transactions/new`.
- Confirm actions with a flash: add the message key to `MESSAGES` in `app/flash.py` and pass it to `htmx_redirect`.
- Account pickers on the transaction forms use `account_options(groups, selected, allow_new=True)` and carry `data-account-select`; **the row shows a button that opens one bottom sheet, `<dialog id="row-picker">`, for accounts and categories alike (2026-10-29; `syncRowPickers` and the picker block in `app.js`: search (no "Used lately": the owner removed it 2026-10-30), group headings, a balance at the right of an Assets or Liabilities option (`data-balance`, "owed" for a liability), the chosen one in bold and no tick circle (one choice closes the sheet), the account the other side already uses greyed, "＋ New account" / "＋ New category"); the `<select>` stays hidden underneath so the kind buttons, payee chips and swap keep working, and the list reserves its scroll bar's space (`scrollbar-gutter`)**; `app.js` opens the `<dialog id="quick-account">` sheet (`/accounts/quick`) when "+ New account…" is chosen, then refreshes every picker from `/accounts/options` on the `account-added` event. Restore pickers synchronously (`cancel` event, cancel buttons), never from the async `close` event alone.
- Icons come from the `icon()` macro in `partials/icons.html`; styles use the CSS variables at the top of `static/style.css` (both light and dark themes).

`app/session.py` signs users in through Supabase's password grant and keeps the tokens in `HttpOnly` cookies. `web_auth.web_login_required` verifies the cookie (refreshing it when expired) and stores the token in `request.state`, which `require_authenticated_user` accepts in addition to a bearer header, so repositories stay tenant-scoped. The JSON API also takes the cookie, for the finode app, with the origin and header rule in `docs/architecture/mcp-and-auth.md`. Sign-up is invite-only: `app/signup.py` checks the invite code (constant time) and creates pre-confirmed users through Supabase's admin API with the service key, which must stay server-side — never render it, log it or send it to the browser. `settings.signup_enabled` gates the routes (404 otherwise). In tests, `tests/conftest.py` overrides both dependencies; use a client without the `web_login_required` override (see `tests/test_web_auth.py`) to test real authentication.

## What an amount field accepts

Every `input[inputmode="decimal"]` (the transaction form, the budget's sheet, move and target, account balances) is cleaned as it is typed or pasted (`cleanAmount` at the top of `app.js`, a capturing `input` listener so the other handlers read the clean value): digits, commas to group them, **one** decimal point (a second one is ignored, so `42.00.00` becomes `42.00`) and a minus sign only first; letters and anything else are dropped and the cursor stays where it was. **Zeros in front are dropped as it is typed** (`01` is 1) and **an amount is written out whole when the field is left or the form is sent** (`tidyAmount` in `app.js`: no commas, the currency's decimals, so `1.` is 1.00 and `.5` is 0.50; an empty field stays empty; 2026-10-24). **Decimals stop at what the currency keeps** (`<html data-places>`, from `templating.places`: rupees 2, yen 0, so a third digit or any point for yen is not typed; 2026-10-24); the server still checks (`check_places`: it normalizes first, so trailing zeros pass and `42.005` in rupees is refused with a message) for the API and pasted values. `parse_amount` refuses anything left that is not a number.

## The amount field's focus

On a new entry the amount carries `data-autofocus` (not `autofocus`): `app.js` focuses it only when the device has a mouse (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`). On a phone nothing is focused, because a script cannot open the keyboard: the grey 0.00 stays visible until the first digit is typed, **also while the field is focused** (the cursor sits at its start, `:placeholder-shown { text-align: left }`; the same on a phone and a computer, 2026-10-24). Tapping a payee chip still focuses the amount (a real tap, so the keyboard opens).

## Lakhs and crores

For INR the whole part of an amount is grouped the Indian way (`12,34,567.00`: the last three digits, then pairs), every other currency in thousands (`1,234,567.00`). `templating.money` follows the person's currency without being told: the `protected` web router loads it for every page (`dependencies.load_currency` puts it in `request.state.currency`), and `templating.TemplateResponse` is wrapped to set a context variable (`_grouping`) while the page renders, so the 60 `|money` call sites did not change. Python code that writes an amount into a string outside a template passes the style (`money(value, grouping_for(code))`, as the budget's hints do). `<html data-grouping="indian|western">` tells `app.js` (`groupDigits`, used by the allocation status under the form's From/To rows). Amounts under one lakh read the same either way (99,999.00). Only the rupee is grouped this way; PKR, BDT and others stay in thousands unless asked.

## The rupee sign and what was added automatically

For INR the unit is the rupee sign, **in front** (`templating.symbol` / `amount`; the `figure` macro in `partials/money.html` for large figures: Home, Budget's Ready to assign; the Add screen's amount has it in front of the digits, which is as wide as what is typed: `--n` set in `recomputeTxn`). Other currencies keep their code after the amount (the person's one currency, see "One currency"; the amounts in lists carry no unit at all). The rupee sign comes from our own font, not the phone's: `anek-latin.woff2` carries U+20B9 and its `unicode-range` names it. What an assistant or a recurring rule added is **violet** (`--auto`, light and dark; it was blue until 2026-11-07, when blue became the action colour): the "Recurring" / "Added by Claude" line under a row (`.tx-via`) and the "Added" and "Repeats" facts in the details sheet (`dd.auto`).

## Signs and the pace notch

Amounts in transaction lists, the details sheet and an account's activity show **no minus on ordinary spending** (the default, so a long list is not a column of signs): income is `+` and green (`.tx-amount.income`), transfers plain. A real negative (an overdrawn account, an overspent category's "left", a debt) keeps its `−` and red, and **in a large figure or `templating.amount` the minus comes after the rupee sign, next to the digits (`₹−1,200.00`; changed 2026-10-25, it used to read `−₹1,200.00`)**; the `money` filter still writes a true minus sign. Every progress bar (Home's `.glance-bar`, the budget's `.crow .bar`) is **3 px thick** (was 6 px until 2026-10-23), from the one token `--bar-h` in `tokens.css`: change it there, never per bar. Bars of the current month carry a **pace notch (a small triangle under the bar) showing how far through the month today is** (`clock.month_progress`, `.pace` inside `.glance-bar` on Home and `.bar` on the budget; `pace` is None for other months, which have no today): a fill ahead of the tick is spending faster than the month passes. **Green and "+" mean money coming in**, which on an account's own page (`_is_inflow` in `routers/web/accounts.py`, offered to the template as `is_inflow`; also used by the upcoming card) is what **raises an asset account** and a **payment into a liability** (a card or loan: it lowers what you owe). A charge on a card is plain, with no sign; the running balance beside each row shows which way it went. Income categories are green, Expenses plain.

## The "i" beside Home's and Budget's figure

A round `i` (`.calc-info`) sits beside the label of **Left to spend** (Home) and **Ready to assign** (Budget) and opens, in place under the figure, the sum behind it with the person's own numbers (`partials/calc_info.html`: `calc_button`, `calc_panel`; shown by CSS `:has(.calc-info[open])`, no script). Home: carried in from earlier months (`glance.carried` = available - assigned + spent) + assigned this month - spent = left. Budget: money in the accounts - set aside for card bills - left in the categories - assigned to later months = ready to assign (`BudgetRead`; the panel shows the true figure, so a negative one reads as it is, while the headline stays at zero). Every row shows even at zero so the column always adds up; a negative amount flips its sign instead of showing a minus. If the budget's arithmetic changes, change the rows here and the two tests that read them.

## Offline, failed saves and loading

`base.html` carries one `#net-banner` (hidden) and a `.loadbar`; the last block of `app.js` drives them. Offline (`navigator.onLine`) shows "You're offline…" until the connection is back; a request that cannot reach the server (`htmx:sendError`), times out or answers 5xx shows a message that says **the entry is still on the page** (forms are never cleared on failure, and 4xx validation messages still swap in as before); a later successful request clears an error. The bar shows while an htmx request or a page load is under way, and a form being sent cannot be sent twice (`form.htmx-request` disables its buttons). There is **no offline queue**: nothing is saved until the connection is back (browser tests in `tests/browser/test_states.py`, run in Chromium and WebKit). Empty states are a short sentence and one next step, without a box.

## Bento tiles

Rows are grouped into soft rounded tiles (`--surface` with a `--tile-border` hairline, a hairline between rows inside, a gap between tiles) so groups are easy to tell apart while the page stays minimal: a tile per day in transaction lists (`.tx-day ul.card.list`), per account type and per group on the accounts page, per group of categories on the budget (`.budget-tile`, opened in `budget_body.html` at each top-level group and at the first plain category after one) and per section in Settings (`.tile`). The tile rule is one block at the end of `style.css`; new lists should reuse it rather than draw their own borders.

## Contrast (2026-11-04)

`--muted` is `#5a6560` in the light theme (5.3 : 1 on the paper, 5.9 : 1 on a tile, 4.8 : 1 on a grey chip) and `#949eb0` in dark (6.8 and 6.2 : 1); `--faint` is `#737e78` (3.7 : 1, for placeholders and glyphs, not for sentences). `tests/test_contrast.py` fails if `--muted` drops under 4.5 : 1 on the page, a tile or a chip in either theme: change the greys only with that test in view.

## The passbook look (2026-11-07)

The look comes from an Indian bank passbook: pale green-grey paper (`--bg` `#ecf0ec`), white slips for tiles (`--surface` `#fbfcfa`), blue-black ink for text (`--text` `#172033`) and **royal-blue ink for whatever you can act on** (`--accent` `#2b3a9e`: buttons, the docked "+", the chosen switcher chip, focus, and every progress bar). Dark is "night ink": a blue-black page (`#10151f`) with a periwinkle accent (`#9db0ff`). Green and red still mean only money in and out, and an overspent bar stays red. In light a tile has no visible line (`--tile-border` is the slip's own colour); in dark it keeps a hairline.

**Type** is Anek Latin (Ek Type, OFL), one variable font with weight 400–700 and width 75–100 % (`static/fonts/anek-latin.woff2`, about 65 KB, Latin plus ₹ and tabular figures; `README.txt` says how it was cut). Text is the normal width; titles and the wordmark are 87.5 %; **the big figures are condensed** (`font-stretch: 75%`, weight 650, 3.375rem: `.glance-figure`, `.ready .figure`, `.hero-balance`, and the Add screen's amount at 3.75rem). That condensed figure is the one loud thing on a page; keep everything else quiet.

**Corners** follow what a thing is: tiles and sheets `--radius` (20px), fields `--radius-sm` (14px), chips, switchers, the kind pill and buttons fully round (999px).

The mock-up the owner approved: https://claude.ai/artifact/GoV9wYTkHZbkuYZNT9vBFp

## Dark mode

Both dark blocks in `tokens.css` (the automatic `prefers-color-scheme` one and the manual `data-theme="dark"` one) must define the same tokens (a test compares them). Use tokens only, never literal colours (the one exception is the white switch knob, which switches to `--on-accent` when on). Form fields use `--field-border` (stronger than the hairline `--border` in dark so empty fields are visible), the big amount placeholder uses `--faint`, and `favicon.svg` is royal ink with a paper mark (periwinkle and night ink in dark, by `prefers-color-scheme`). `apple-touch-icon.png` is still the older blue mark.

## One design system (2026-10-23)

Every page is made of the same parts, so none looks like it came from another app. **Switcher:** one segmented control for every "which list" (`.chips.seg` with `a.chip[aria-pressed]`: Assets · Liabilities, Expense · Income, the Transactions kinds; the Add screen's Expense · Income · Transfer is the same look, `.segmented.kinds`); the bin and "+" icons stay at the right of the switcher row. **Row:** a title, a quiet second line, the figure at the right; an entry with no payee leads with where the money went and the second line says where it came from (`transaction_list.html`, `account_entries.html`, the upcoming card); Budgets rows have "of X" beside the name so each is one line plus the bar. **Form:** boxed fields in one card, From and To on the Add screen are tiles (`.txn-side`), the kind control comes first, and a form page ends in **one full-width primary button with no Cancel** (the back link is the cancel; sheets keep theirs). **Empty state:** a sentence and one pill button (`.empty-state`). **Home's overspent warning** is one quiet line, not a box. **Account page:** no Add button; the docked "+" is the way (2026-11-04). New pages should reuse these parts, not draw their own. **Day labels** in transaction lists carry what was spent that day at the right ("Spent 12.50", expenses only, and only on a day with more than one entry; `.tx-day-total`, `transaction_list.html`); Home's label row is centred on the month pill and as tall as the other pages' title rows.

**Undo was removed (2026-11-05)** with the history (see [removed.md](removed.md)): the toast is a message only. **What needs a decision** sits under the bell in the top bar (2026-10-30; it was a "N things need you" line on Budgets until then).

**Loading, quick amounts and the wide Home (2026-10-23).** A sheet (transaction, category) opens **at once with grey placeholders** (`.skeleton`, `SHEET_SKELETON` in `app.js`, on `htmx:beforeRequest`) and the real content replaces them; if the request fails the sheet closes again, and a sheet the person closed while it loaded stays closed (the close drops the request with `htmx:abort`, so nothing arrives to reopen it). Pages fade in for 140 ms (`main { animation: page-in }`; both off under reduced motion). The quick amounts under the amount on a new entry (+/−, steps, multipliers, undo, and "₹597 as last time") were **all removed on 2026-10-24** to keep the MVP simple: people type the amount. ~~From 60rem Home is two columns~~ (removed 2026-10-24): every page is one column.

**Pace notch (2026-10-23).** The month marker on every bar (`.pace`) is a small triangle *under* the bar, not a vertical line across it (the owner disliked the `|`); same markup and rules as before.

**Home's head has room to breathe (2026-10-23).** Looser gaps between the label row, figure, bar, warning and the Transactions heading (a block at the end of `style.css`, scoped to `.glance` and Home's `.section-head`); no parts changed.

**The month selector has no box (2026-10-23).** `.month-pill` is plain text (arrows and the month, no border or fill) on every page that uses the macro; the tap targets and the native picker are unchanged.

**Faint ⓘ (2026-10-23).** The round `i` beside Left to spend and Ready to assign is `--faint` until hovered or opened (`.calc-info .info-button`); the account note's "i" is unchanged.

**The month selector sits on the same line everywhere (2026-10-23).** Home's head no longer has extra padding above the label row, so the selector is at the same height as on Budgets, Categories and Transactions (phone and wide: `test_the_month_pill_sits_on_the_same_line_on_home_and_the_other_pages`). Home is one column on every screen width (the wide two-column Home was removed 2026-10-24 at the owner's request).

**Full-width switchers, "Add" at the end, deleted things in Settings (2026-10-23).** On Accounts and Categories the switcher spans the page as on Budgets (the round + and the bin left the row; `.head-icons` stays on Transactions). Adding is the dashed `.add-row` after the list (it was `.goal-add`),.

**Transactions has a full-width switcher too (2026-10-23).** The bin and the repeat icon left the kind chips' row; **Recurring transactions** is reached only from Settings › Your data. `.head-icons` is no longer used by any page.

**Motion (2026-10-23).** Switching with a chip uses the browser's own page transition, in CSS only (`@view-transition { navigation: auto; }` at the end of `style.css`): the chosen chip (`view-transition-name: switch-pill`) glides to its new place in .28 s, the title row and switcher (`.page-head`, `.cat-bar`) stay still, and everything under them fades in with a 6 px rise (`rise-in`, .22 s, on every page). Reduced motion turns it all off; browsers without view transitions just get the rise. The browser tests run with reduced motion so measured positions never catch a page mid-rise.

**A press that starts inside a sheet never closes it (2026-10-24).** Focusing the amount reshapes the sheet, so the release could land on the dim area and the click closed the sheet: tapping a field felt hit and miss (worse on a taller sheet, such as a subcategory's). `pressedInside` in `app.js` ignores the click on the dim area when the press began on the sheet's content.

**The kind switcher glides (2026-10-24).** On the Add screen the Expense / Income / Transfer pill is one `::before` pill that slides to the chosen button (`form[data-txn-kind]` picks the position, transform and opacity only so Safari on iOS and macOS animate it; it fades out for "other"); the buttons no longer paint their own selected background. Off under reduced motion.

**Safari on iOS and the keyboard's form bar (2026-11-04).** The translucent bar above the keyboard is not counted by the visual viewport, so a single filtered row sat under it. While a field in a sheet has focus on an iPhone or iPad, `place()` sets `--kb-bar` (.75rem on top of the safe-area padding the sheet already has; 0 elsewhere) and a bottom sheet keeps that much padding below its content.

**Sheets and toasts move (2026-10-23).** A bottom sheet slides up from the edge in .25 s and the dim area fades (CSS `@starting-style` and `transition-behavior` on `dialog.sheet`, end of `style.css`; closing runs it backwards); on wide screens the sheet rises 12 px and fades. A toast rises in over .25 s and fades out (`toast-in`, `toast-out`, `--toast-life`: 2.9 s). The sheet itself takes no focus ring (`outline: none`, for iOS). Reduced motion removes the sheet's transition and keeps the toast's timing without movement.

**Bars, folds and presses (2026-10-23).** Bars (`.glance-bar i`, `.crow .bar i`) fill from zero in .5 s when a page loads (`bar-fill`, a few ms of stagger by row); `app.js` sets `html[data-live]` after 900 ms and the rule only applies without it, so a bar swapped in by assigning an amount does not replay. Folding rows (`details.upcoming`) ease their height through `::details-content` with `interpolate-size` where the browser can and fade their content in everywhere (`rise-in`; the ⓘ sum uses the same rise). Tappable rows (`a.tx`, `a.crow`, `a.up-row`, `a.setting-link`, `a.add-row`) dim while pressed, buttons dim and shrink 2 %; the release eases back. All of it is off under reduced motion.

**Content fades (2026-10-23).** What a sheet loads (`#txn-sheet-body`, `#cat-sheet-body`, direct children) fades in over its grey placeholders in .18 s. (A saved row used to settle in with a tint; that needed the Undo button's id and went with it.)

The **Under** field of the account and category forms is a button (`.field-pick[data-pick-generic]`) over a hidden `select[data-sheet-select]`, opening the same `#row-picker` sheet as the Add screen (`partials/pick_dialog.html`, included by `txn_rows.html`, `account_form.html` and `category_form.html`; the sheet's `flat` mode lists accounts as one plain list, each with its group in small type; `data-allow-empty` adds "None (top level)" for categories). ## The details panel on a wide screen (2026-11-04)

From 80rem with a mouse (`(min-width: 80rem) and (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, `wideScreen` in `app.js`) a row's details sheet (`#txn-sheet`: Transactions, Home, an account's and a category's page) opens as a **panel beside the list**: `openSheet` calls `dialog.show()` (not `showModal()`) and adds `.side-panel` (fixed at the right, 22rem, a × added by the script, Esc closes it); while one is open `body.app:has(dialog.side-panel[open]) main` moves left and narrows so the list is never under it, a tap on another row replaces the panel and the open row carries `a.tx.is-open`. Phones, touch screens and narrower windows keep the bottom sheet, and every other sheet (filter, pickers) stays modal. **The budget's category sheet (`#cat-sheet`) is a panel too (2026-11-05)**: a category row (`a.crow`) carries `is-open` while its panel is open, typing in the amount does not fold the panel up (`.typing:not(.side-panel)`), and saving swaps `#budget` so the panel closes. Tests: `tests/browser/test_side_panel.py`.
