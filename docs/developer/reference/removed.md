# Removed on purpose

Features the owner asked to remove. Do not bring any of them back without the owner asking. Dates in headings are when a decision was made. Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-07; the rules summary is in `AGENTS.md`.

## No history (2026-11-05, `feature/remove-history`)

finode keeps **no history of what happened to a transaction or an account**, at the owner's request ("I don't want to maintain any kind of history"). The tables `transaction_history`, `account_history` and `account_deletions` are gone (migration `b7d9f1a3c5e2`; its downgrade brings back empty tables), and with them: the Undo button on the toast, the Recently deleted pages (`/transactions/deleted`, `/accounts/deleted`, `/categories/deleted`, the Settings row and their restore routes), the change logs (`/api/transactions/{id}/changes`, `/history`, `/changes`, `/api/accounts/.../changes`, `/api/.../deleted` and `.../restore`) and the assistants' `get_transaction_history`, `get_account_history`, `recent_changes`, `list_deleted_transactions` and `restore_transaction`. **Deleting is for good**: the delete buttons ask first (`hx-confirm`, "This cannot be undone"). What stays is *who*: `transactions.created_via` and `updated_via` (the assistant marker in lists and the details sheet) and the per-row `origin` that merges write. A closed category or account keeps its entries and is reopened with `POST /accounts/{account_id}/reopen` / `/categories/{category_id}/reopen`; there is no list of closed categories any more (a closed account is under "Load more" on Accounts). Do not add a log, snapshot or undo again without the owner asking.

## No Advanced switch (removed 2026-10-24)

`profiles.advanced` and the Advanced page are gone (migration `a9b1c3d5e7f2` drops the column; the downgrade adds it back, off). What it hid is gone or always there: Close account is on an account page's menu (`account.html`); Move up/down and the period lock were removed (2026-10-25). Home's "Get started" card stays while steps are left and the Budgets welcome until the first amount is assigned, for everybody. Old backups' `profile.advanced` is read and ignored (`exclude=True`); `ProfileUpdate`/`ProfileRead` no longer carry it.

## No period lock (removed 2026-10-25)

`profiles.locked_through`, `services/lock.py`, the Settings row, the edit page's notice and the budget's closed months are gone (migration `b2d4f6a8c0e3` drops the column; the downgrade adds it back, empty). Nothing refuses an entry for its date. Older backups' `locked_through` is read and ignored.

**Targets and notes were removed (2026-10-24; nobody used them).** The budget has no per-category targets (monthly, weekly, refill, by-date, yearly), no "Assign what is needed" (`/budget/fund`, `BudgetService.fund`), no week-by-week view and no note under a category's name: `budget_targets` is dropped (migration `f2c4e6a8b0d1`, which also has a downgrade that brings back an empty table), the API's `PUT/DELETE /api/budget/categories/{account_id}/target`, the web target page (`/budget/target`, `/budget/note`) and the assistants' `set_budget_target` and `fund_budget_targets` are gone, and older backups' `targets` are read and ignored (`BackupFile.targets` is `exclude=True`). A category's own text (`accounts.details`) stays on its account page. `BudgetRead.underfunded` is gone, and so is the `funding()` rule goals used.

**Goals were removed (2026-11-05, `feature/remove-goals`).** The Save chip (savings goals with money set aside month by month, "Use it for…", the New goal form and sheets) is gone, with the `goals` and `goal_allocations` tables (migration `a6c8e0b2d4f1`; its downgrade brings back empty tables), `BudgetRead.goals` and the three `goal_*` sums, the `/api/budget/goals` routes and the assistants' `create_goal`, `update_goal`, `delete_goal`, `assign_to_goal` and `use_goal`. Budgets has no chips now (the page is the budget alone, `/budget?month=`; there is no `tab`), the identity is `ready_to_assign = money_in_accounts − reserved_for_cards − available − assigned_to_later_months`, and a backup file that still has `goals` restores without them (the field is no longer read). Do not add savings to the budget again without the owner asking.
