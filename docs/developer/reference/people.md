# People (contacts, sharing, settlements)

Contacts and the accounts behind them, transfers, shared expenses, settlements, opening balances and the People pages. Dates in headings are when a decision was made. Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-07; the rules summary is in `AGENTS.md`.

## Contacts, expense sharing and settlements (2026-11-06, branch `feature/contacts`; plan, phases and deviations in `docs/developer/contacts-plan.md`)

A **contact** (`contacts`: `id`, `user_id`, `name` up to 30 characters and unique per person capitals aside, `email`, `phone`, `notes`, `archived_at`) is a person the owner lends to, borrows from or shares costs with. It is **not an account**: its ledger side is two ordinary accounts made with it through `AccountService.create(..., commit=False)`, **"Due from X"** (an asset directly under Assets) and **"Due to X"** (a liability directly under Liabilities), both `on_budget=False`, linked in `contact_accounts` (unique per contact and role, per account). What a contact owes is read from those accounts' balances (entries dated ahead included, `LAST_DAY`), never stored. The account service refuses to change, close, delete or merge them (`_refuse_contact`, `AccountRepository.contact_account_ids`); rename follows the contact (`rename_contact_account`). `ContactService` (`services/contact.py`): create, update, archive (refused while either account holds a balance), restore, delete (removes a contact nothing was ever done with, else archives: `ContactDeleteResult.outcome`), list (`q`, `owing`, `sort`), `own_account` (an asset or liability of the owner's, not a contact's). `FINODE_MAX_CONTACTS` (200).

**The rule that keeps the books honest:** `TransactionService(contacts=True)` (built by `dependencies.get_contact_transactions`, used only by the contact services) is the only one that may post to a contact's accounts or edit/delete an entry the contact services made (an entry with a `party_transactions` row). The ordinary routes, recurring rules, imports through the service and the assistants' `record_*` tools answer 400 ("belongs to a contact"). `TransactionService.create/delete(commit=False)` let a workflow save everything in one commit; the contact services never write postings themselves.

**Tables:** `party_transactions` (contact, entry, purpose loan|borrowing|advance|gift|expense|settlement, direction sent|received: the activity), `party_obligations` (one amount owed: contact, source entry, source_type shared_expense|loan|borrowing|advance, direction receivable|payable, `original_amount`; never changed), `obligation_settlements` (obligation, settling entry, amount), `contact_requests` (idempotency key → entry), `shared_expenses` (+ `expense_shares`, `expense_contributions`). **Outstanding = original − Σ settlements, always computed** (`ObligationService`), never stored; for a contact `Σ outstanding receivables = Due from balance` and likewise payables (`tests/test_contact_summary.py` checks it after random operations). Migrations `c8e0a2b4d6f3`, `d9f1b3c5e7a4`, `e0a2c4d6f8b5`.

**Transfers** (`services/transfer.py`, `POST /api/contacts/{id}/transfers`): sent loan/advance = debit Due from, credit your account (a receivable); received borrowing/advance = debit your account, credit Due to (a payable); gift = spending (sent) or income (received) under a category, nothing owed. The budget sees only the money in the accounts (a loan is not spending); `BudgetService` leaves contact accounts out of "uncategorised payments" and "add to budget". **Shared expenses** (`services/expense_sharing.py`, `services/splits.py`): a draft (shares worked out by `equal|exact|percentage|shares` with the largest-remainder method, so shares add up exactly) is confirmed into one entry: debit Expenses (your share, under the expense's category), credit what you paid, and per contact the difference between what they paid and their share (debit Due from if less, credit Due to if more; nobody owes a third contact). Confirming needs the bill fully paid for; only your share reaches your budget. A confirmed expense is not edited, only cancelled (deleted with its entry and obligations; refused once something was settled). **Settlements** (`services/settlement.py`): `received` clears receivables, `sent` payables; `fifo` (oldest entry first) or `manual` allocations; never beyond what is owed (rows are locked with `FOR UPDATE` and the owed figures re-read inside the transaction); the rest must be named (`excess_as`: loan/borrowing/advance make a new obligation, gift is spending/income); paying back a share of a bill a friend paid labels the payment with the expense's category when the original entry touched no budget account (that is when the spending reaches the budget). Reversal of a transfer, expense or settlement deletes it for good (no history), refused (409) while something was settled against what it left owed. **Idempotency:** the `Idempotency-Key` header on transfers, settlements and `shared-expenses` (confirm) returns the first result on a retry (409 if the key was used for a different request); keys die with their entry. **Summary:** `ContactSummaryService` (`/summary`, `/activity`).

**Web (phase 6, `routers/web/people.py`, mock-ups chosen by the owner 2026-11-07):** People lives in **Settings › Your data** and the wide-screen sidebar (no fifth tab): `/people` (chips All · Owe you · You owe · Archived; the rows are the Categories rows), `/people/{id}` (the net as one figure, **Add expense / Lend or borrow / Settle up**, the open amounts, the activity whose rows open `#person-sheet`, the … menu), forms as pages (`/people/new`, `/edit`, `/transfer` with a tile of six kinds and a gift-only category shown by CSS `:has`, `/settle` with a live preview `partials/settle_preview.html` that asks what the rest is, `/people/expense/new` with a live `partials/expense_preview.html`; previews are an htmx `hx-trigger="change from:closest form, input delay:400ms from:closest form"` div). The sheets of the mock-ups became pages (fewer sheets over sheets). No drafts in the UI (the API has them). The contact accounts are hidden from the Accounts page, the pickers and the Filter sheet (`without_contacts`, `AccountRead.contact_account`; the flag comes in the same query as the account list); a transaction sheet for an entry made through a person says so and links to the person (`with_person`). Tests: `tests/test_web_people.py`, `tests/browser/test_people.py`.

**People on the Add screen (2026-11-07, `feature/people-in-transfer`, option B of the owner's mock-ups):** on a new entry the Transfer (and "other") pickers get a **People** group (an `optgroup` labelled `People` whose option values are contact ids, `data-person`; `people_group` in `routers/web/transactions.py`, only when the person has contacts, so nobody else pays a query; never on the edit page or the recurring form). A person on the To side is a **loan** (money out), on the From side a **borrowing** (money in); `POST /transactions/split` sees a contact id among the rows and hands the entry to `TransferService.record` (`_transfer_with_person`: exactly one person and one of your accounts, the same amount, no tags or related entry), so it is exactly what the person's page records. A note under the form says what it does (`[data-person-note]`). Paying back and being paid back stay on the person's page (Settle up). `KINDS.transfer` in `app.js` lists `People` among its roots.

**Opening balances (2026-11-07, `feature/people-opening-balance`, option A of the owner's mock-ups):** a person can already have owed (`direction` receivable) or been owed (payable) before finode: `TransferService.opening_balance`, `POST /api/contacts/{id}/opening-balance`, one entry dated `date` with the payee "Opening balance" against the system Opening Balances account (debit "Due from X" / credit Opening Balances, or debit Opening Balances / credit "Due to X"), activity purpose `opening`, obligation source `opening` (migration `f1b3d5e7a9c6` widens the two CHECKs; its downgrade turns them into a loan or a borrowing). None of the owner's accounts or budgets move; it is settled (oldest first) and reversed (`DELETE /api/transfers/{id}`) like a transfer. Web: a folded row "Already owes you, or is owed?" on Add person (checked before the person is made; if recording fails the new person is removed again) and "Add an opening balance" in a person's … menu (`/people/{id}/opening`).

**API:** `/api/contacts/...` (list, create, get, patch, delete, archive, restore, `obligations`, `transfers`, `settlements`, `settlements/preview`, `summary`, `activity`), `/api/transfers/{transaction_id}`, `/api/settlements/{transaction_id}`, `/api/shared-expenses/...` (`preview`, create with `confirm`, list, get, patch, `confirm`, `cancel`). Isolation parameter names: `contact_id`, `expense_id`, `obligation_id`; `transaction_id` for transfers and settlements. **Backup** is version 6 (contacts, party transactions, obligations, settlements, shared expenses; older files restore without them); Erase removes every table (all carry `user_id`). Do not add history or audit tables for contacts (see "No history").
