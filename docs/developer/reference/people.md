# People (contacts, sharing, settlements)

Contacts and the accounts behind them, transfers, shared expenses, settlements, opening balances and the People pages. Dates in headings are when a decision was made. Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-07; the rules summary is in `AGENTS.md`.

## Contacts, expense sharing and settlements (2026-11-06, branch `feature/contacts`; plan, phases and deviations in `docs/developer/contacts-plan.md`)

A **contact** (`contacts`: `id`, `user_id`, `name` up to 30 characters and unique per person capitals aside, `email`, `phone`, `notes`, `archived_at`) is a person the owner lends to, borrows from or shares costs with. It is **not an account**: its ledger side is two ordinary accounts made with it through `AccountService.create(..., commit=False)`, **"Due from X"** (an asset directly under Assets) and **"Due to X"** (a liability directly under Liabilities), both `on_budget=False`, linked in `contact_accounts` (unique per contact and role, per account). What a contact owes is read from those accounts' balances (entries dated ahead included, `LAST_DAY`), never stored. The account service refuses to change, close, delete or merge them (`_refuse_contact`, `AccountRepository.contact_account_ids`); rename follows the contact (`rename_contact_account`). `ContactService` (`services/contact.py`): create, update, archive (refused while either account holds a balance), restore, delete (removes a contact nothing was ever done with, else archives: `ContactDeleteResult.outcome`), list (`q`, `owing`, `sort`), `own_account` (an asset or liability of the owner's, not a contact's). `FINODE_MAX_CONTACTS` (200).

**The rule that keeps the books honest:** `TransactionService(contacts=True)` (built by `dependencies.get_contact_transactions`, used only by the contact services) is the only one that may post to a contact's accounts or edit/delete an entry the contact services made (an entry with a `party_transactions` row). The ordinary routes, recurring rules, imports through the service and the assistants' `record_*` tools answer 400 ("belongs to a contact"). `TransactionService.create/delete(commit=False)` let a workflow save everything in one commit; the contact services never write postings themselves.

**Tables:** `party_transactions` (contact, entry, purpose loan|borrowing|advance|gift|expense|settlement, direction sent|received: the activity), `party_obligations` (one amount owed: contact, source entry, source_type shared_expense|loan|borrowing|advance, direction receivable|payable, `original_amount`; never changed), `obligation_settlements` (obligation, settling entry, amount), `contact_requests` (idempotency key → entry), `shared_expenses` (+ `expense_shares`, `expense_contributions`). **Outstanding = original − Σ settlements, always computed** (`ObligationService`), never stored; for a contact `Σ outstanding receivables = Due from balance` and likewise payables (`tests/test_contact_summary.py` checks it after random operations). Migrations `c8e0a2b4d6f3`, `d9f1b3c5e7a4`, `e0a2c4d6f8b5`.

**Transfers** (`services/transfer.py`, `POST /api/contacts/{id}/transfers`): sent loan/advance = debit Due from, credit your account (a receivable); received borrowing/advance = debit your account, credit Due to (a payable); gift = spending (sent) or income (received) under a category, nothing owed. The budget sees only the money in the accounts (a loan is not spending); `BudgetService` leaves contact accounts out of "uncategorised payments" and "add to budget". **Shared expenses** (`services/expense_sharing.py`, `services/splits.py`): a draft (shares worked out by `equal|exact|percentage|shares` with the largest-remainder method, so shares add up exactly) is confirmed into one entry: debit Expenses (your share, under the expense's category), credit what you paid, and per contact the difference between what they paid and their share (debit Due from if less, credit Due to if more; nobody owes a third contact). Confirming needs the bill fully paid for; only your share reaches your budget. A confirmed expense is not edited, only cancelled (deleted with its entry and obligations; refused once something was settled). **Settlements** (`services/settlement.py`): `received` clears receivables, `sent` payables; `fifo` (oldest entry first) or `manual` allocations; never beyond what is owed (rows are locked with `FOR UPDATE` and the owed figures re-read inside the transaction); the rest must be named (`excess_as`: loan/borrowing/advance make a new obligation, gift is spending/income); paying back a share of a bill a friend paid labels the payment with the expense's category when the original entry touched no budget account (that is when the spending reaches the budget). Reversal of a transfer, expense or settlement deletes it for good (no history), refused (409) while something was settled against what it left owed. **Idempotency:** the `Idempotency-Key` header on transfers, settlements and `shared-expenses` (confirm) returns the first result on a retry (409 if the key was used for a different request); keys die with their entry. **Summary:** `ContactSummaryService` (`/summary`, `/activity`).

**Web (phase 6, `routers/web/people.py`, mock-ups chosen by the owner 2026-11-07):** People lives in **Settings › Your data** and the wide-screen sidebar (no fifth tab): `/people` (chips All · Owe you · You owe · Archived; the rows are the Categories rows), `/people/{id}` (the net as one figure, the pills **Expense · Lend · Borrow · Settle**, the open amounts that open their entry, the activity whose rows open `#person-sheet`, the … menu), forms as pages (`/people/new`, `/edit`, the opening balance). **The pills record nothing** (2026-11-11): each links to `/transactions/new` with `with=<person>` and `back=/people/<id>`: Expense is `mode=split`, Lend `move=lend` (person on the To side), Borrow `move=borrow` (From), Settle `move=settle` (the side that clears what is owed, with `total` set to it; only shown while something is owed). The earlier Lend-or-borrow, Settle-up (with its live preview and single-amount payoff) and Net-off pages, the six "kinds" and the `/people/{id}/transfer|settle|netoff` routes are gone; `/people/expense/new` and its preview (`partials/expense_preview.html`) stay. The contact accounts are hidden from the Accounts page, the pickers and the Filter sheet (`without_contacts`, `AccountRead.contact_account`; the flag comes in the same query as the account list); a transaction sheet for an entry made through a person says so and links to the person (`with_person`). Tests: `tests/test_web_people.py`, `tests/test_people_through_plus.py`, `tests/browser/test_people.py`.

**People on the Add screen (2026-11-07, `feature/people-in-transfer`, option B of the owner's mock-ups):** on a new entry the Transfer (and "other") pickers get a **People** group (an `optgroup` labelled `People` whose option values are contact ids, `data-person`; `people_group` in `routers/web/transactions.py`, only when the person has contacts, so nobody else pays a query; never on the edit page or the recurring form). A person on the To side is a **loan** (money out), on the From side a **borrowing** (money in); `POST /transactions/split` sees a contact id among the rows and hands the entry to `TransferService.record` (`_transfer_with_person`: exactly one person and one of your accounts, the same amount, no tags or related entry), so it is exactly what the person's page records. A note under the form says what it does (`[data-person-note]`). Paying back and being paid back are the same transfer (below). `KINDS.transfer` in `app.js` lists `People` among its roots.

**Money to or from a person is one transfer (2026-11-11):** `TransferService.move` (used by `POST /transactions/split` for a transfer with a person; the API's explicit `transfers` and `settlements` are unchanged): money *from* a person who owes you pays that back first (oldest first, through `SettlementService.record`, the extra becoming a borrowing via `excess_as`), money *to* a person you owe does the same with a loan for the extra; with nothing owed that way it is a plain loan or borrowing. One entry either way. The transfer has **no Simple/Split switch** any more (`syncModes` offers it on expense and income only) and always lists People; the note under the form (`noteForPeople` in `app.js`, from `data-owes` on each person option = what they owe you, negative when you owe them) says what the amount will do before saving. `people_group` therefore reads `ContactService.list` (one balances query) when the person has contacts.

**A gift names the person as the payee (2026-11-11):** there is no Gift (or Advance) kind on the web: a gift is an ordinary expense or income. Typing `@` in the payee field lists people (`GET /transactions/suggest?payee=@An`, A to Z, archived left out; `?only=people` on the Edit screen, which never suggests payee names); choosing one sets the payee and a hidden `payee_contact`, and saving calls `TransferService.name_gift` in the same commit, adding a `party_transactions` row with purpose `gift` (direction `sent` for an expense, `received` for an income) and no obligation. A gift row does **not** make the entry the contact's: `TransactionRepository.owned_by_contacts` ignores purpose `gift`, so the entry is edited and deleted on the ordinary screens (deleting also removes the row); `made_through_people` (routers/web/transactions.py) leaves gift rows out for the sheet and the Edit screen, which shows the person under the payee with a cross. Typing over the name, leaving expense/income or switching to Split takes the person off (`setPayeePerson`). A person can be named on an expense or an income only. The API's `purpose=gift` transfers are the same kind of entry now. Advances exist in the API only: on the web an advance is a Split in which the other person pays nothing.

**Opening balances (2026-11-07, `feature/people-opening-balance`, option A of the owner's mock-ups):** a person can already have owed (`direction` receivable) or been owed (payable) before finode: `TransferService.opening_balance`, `POST /api/contacts/{id}/opening-balance`, one entry dated `date` with the payee "Opening balance" against the system Opening Balances account (debit "Due from X" / credit Opening Balances, or debit Opening Balances / credit "Due to X"), activity purpose `opening`, obligation source `opening` (migration `f1b3d5e7a9c6` widens the two CHECKs; its downgrade turns them into a loan or a borrowing). None of the owner's accounts or budgets move; it is settled (oldest first) and reversed (`DELETE /api/transfers/{id}`) like a transfer. Web: a folded row "Already owes you, or is owed?" on Add person (checked before the person is made; if recording fails the new person is removed again) and "Add an opening balance" in a person's … menu (`/people/{id}/opening`).

**API:** `/api/contacts/...` (list, create, get, patch, delete, archive, restore, `obligations`, `transfers`, `settlements`, `settlements/preview`, `summary`, `activity`), `/api/transfers/{transaction_id}`, `/api/settlements/{transaction_id}`, `/api/shared-expenses/...` (`preview`, create with `confirm`, list, get, patch, `confirm`, `cancel`). Isolation parameter names: `contact_id`, `expense_id`, `obligation_id`; `transaction_id` for transfers and settlements. **Backup** is version 6 (contacts, party transactions, obligations, settlements, shared expenses; older files restore without them); Erase removes every table (all carry `user_id`). Do not add history or audit tables for contacts (see "No history").

**Editing an opening balance (2026-11-07, `feature/edit-opening-balance`, option A of the owner's mock-ups):** `PATCH /api/transfers/{transaction_id}` / `TransferService.update_opening` rewrites the same entry (new postings, date, note) and the obligation (`original_amount`, `direction`; the one place an obligation changes) in one commit, under a row lock on the obligation. Once something was settled against it the amount cannot go below that (`InsufficientOutstandingBalanceError`, 400) and the direction cannot change (`ConflictError`, 409). Accounts and budgets do not move. Only opening balances are editable: any other transfer is reversed and recorded again. Web: **Edit** in the entry sheet of an opening balance (`/people/{id}/opening/{transaction_id}/edit`, the same form as adding, with a warning when part was paid back).

**Editing an entry into a loan, and back (2026-11-08, `feature/convert-to-person`):** the Edit screen's pickers have the **People** group too (on every entry, not only new ones). Saving with a person on one side calls `TransferService.convert` (`POST /transactions/{id}/edit` in `routers/web/transactions.py`; `_person_rows` is the shared parser): the **same entry** keeps its id, tags and related link, its lines move to the person's account, the payee becomes the person's name, and it gets the activity row and obligation of a loan (person on To) or borrowing (From). An entry that already is a loan or borrowing may change person, side, amount, account, day or note. Saving a loan or borrowing with **no** person on it calls `TransferService.detach` first (activity row, obligation and idempotency key go; the ordinary update then saves; any failure rolls it all back so it stays the loan it was). Both are refused with a message once anything was settled against the loan, and anything else a person made (gift, shared bill, settlement, opening balance) is not editable here (`GET /transactions/{id}/edit` redirects to the person's page; the sheet shows "Open Alice"; a loan's sheet keeps Edit and drops Repeat and Delete). Web only: through the API a loan is reversed and recorded again.

**Evening out (2026-11-11, `feature/people-through-plus`; replaces the manual Net off of 2026-11-08):** a person is never owed both ways. Every service that saves an obligation (`TransferService.record/opening_balance/convert/update_opening`, `SettlementService.record` for its excess, `ExpenseSharingService` and `IncomeSharingService` confirm/convert) calls `NettingService.even_out` (`services/netting.py`) before its commit: if the person now owes you and you owe them, the smaller side is cleared in full and the same amount of the larger, **oldest first on each side**, with one entry (debit "Due to X", credit "Due from X": no account or budget of the owner's moves) dated the day of the later entry it settles, plus a settlement row per obligation touched; activity purpose `netoff`, shown as **Evened out**. `NettingService.release` is the way back: before anything that entry cleared is undone (reversing a loan, borrowing, settlement, bill or shared income, `detach`/`convert`, editing an opening balance) the evening-out entries on it are deleted, the caller does its work and evens out again. The evening-out entry has no reverse of its own (the sheet offers none). The manual page, `GET|POST /contacts/{id}/net-off`, `/net-offs/{id}` and `NetOffRead` are gone (nothing could be owed both ways for them to act on). People who were owed both ways when this shipped were evened out once, by hand, at deploy time.

**Two ways to record an expense or income (2026-11-08, `feature/entry-modes`, reduced to two on `fix/switch-rows` because Simple and Split-as-rows looked almost the same; "+" is the one entry point for every record):** the New and Edit screens have a switch under Expense / Income / Transfer, **Simple · Split** (`partials/txn_rows.html`, radios named `mode`, values `simple` and `split`, `form[data-mode]`; `applyMode`, `syncModes` in `app.js`; it is the same pill-and-glide look as the kind switch, `.scope-seg`, which the Equally / Amounts / % / Shares row and the other segmented choices share). *Simple* is the allocation rows: one a side or more (typing less than the total in a row adds the next); `form[data-layout]` (`single` or `multi`, set by `recomputeTxn`) decides whether "left to allocate" and Remove show. *Split* is sharing with people. On an expense it hides the rows and shows `partials/people_editor.html` (`.people-editor`): the category, the account you paid from, **Equally / Amounts / % / Shares**, **You** and the people added (a row each with a value box and a cross; **Add people** opens `partials/people_sheet.html`, `#people-picker`, the same bottom sheet as the account pickers, with a search and a tick per active person; people are A to Z), and "Someone else paid some of it" (`paid_self`, `paid_<id>`), with a live result from `POST /people/expense/preview` (`expense_data` in `routers/web/people.py` builds the `SharedExpenseCreate`); on an income it shows the income editor (below); a transfer has no such switch since 2026-11-11 (its pickers always list People). The rows are hidden by `form[data-shared]` (set for an expense or income in Split), not by the mode. Each person has a checkbox in the editor (`data-person-toggle`: the form field `with_<id>` on an expense, an unnamed one on an income that only says the row shows); the sheet and the cross flip it, rows with it unchecked are hidden by CSS, and what depends on who is in it (`data-for-person`: who paid, who received) follows (`syncPeople` in `app.js`). Entering the tab copies the account and the category the rows already had, once. Opened from a person's page (`/transactions/new?mode=split&with=<id>`) it starts on the tab with them ticked. `POST /transactions/split` and `POST /transactions/{id}/edit` branch on `mode=split` and `kind`: an expense goes to `ExpenseSharingService.create(confirm=True, comment, tags, related_id)` (new) or `.convert(...)` **in place** (existing: it keeps its id, date, tags and related link; a loan, bill or shared income that was there is released first, `_release`, and everything rolls back if the new records cannot be made); an income to `IncomeSharingService`; a transfer with a person on one side goes to `TransferService.move` whatever the mode says. Saving with *Simple* turns a bill, shared income or loan back into an ordinary entry. Refused once anything was paid back against what the old records left owed. The Edit screen of a bill, shared income or loan opens on Split as entered. Gifts, settlements, opening balances and net offs are still the person's page. Web only.

**Income shared with people (2026-11-08, `feature/entry-matrix`):** money that came in, part of it not yours. `services/income_sharing.py` (`IncomeSharingService`, schemas in `schemas/shared_income.py`, routes `/api/shared-incomes/`) records one ordinary entry: your part is a credit to Income under the category (only that is income), a part that is a contact's is held for them (credit "Due to X", a payable, source `shared_income`), and the money lands in your accounts (debits) or a contact received it for you (debit "Due from X", a receivable). `theirs`, `received_by` and `deposits` are plain amounts (no split methods); `deposits` plus `received_by` must equal the total, a contact is on one side only, at least one is named, the category must be an income one. There is no draft and no table of its own: the activity rows have purpose `income` (direction `received` for a part held, `sent` for money received for you) and the obligations source `shared_income` (migration `b4e6f8a0c2d5` widens the two CHECKs; downgrading turns them into `advance`). It is read back from those rows and the entry's lines (`read`), changed by `convert` (detach, then save onto the same entry; refused once anything was paid back, like a loan) and reversed by `cancel` from the person's entry sheet (`people.py` `reverse_entry`). Web: `partials/income_editor.html` (`data-people-editor="income"`, fields `income_category_id`, `income_account_id`, `theirs_<id>`, `got_<id>`), `income_data` and `POST /people/income/preview` in `routers/web/people.py`, `income_values` and the `kind=income` branch of `/transactions/split` and `/{id}/edit` in `routers/web/transactions.py`; the Edit screen of a shared income opens on Split. Income's sides read "Earned from" / "Paid into" (also on the transaction sheet). Settlements need nothing new: they clear a `payable` or `receivable` whatever its source; `ContactSummaryService` shows the person's part as the activity amount.
