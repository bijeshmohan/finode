# The budget

Envelope budgeting on top of the ledger, the Budgets page, categories, Coming up and the to-do bell. Dates in headings are when a decision was made. Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-07; the rules summary is in `AGENTS.md`.

## The Budgets page (was the Plan, and the Budget page before)

**The Budgets page (2026-10-21; Build and Earn removed 2026-10-22; renamed 2026-10-23).** The page, the tab and the sidebar say **Budgets** (no chips since goals went, 2026-11-05); the web address is `/budget` (`routers/web/budget.py`, `prefix="/budget"`, the templates `budget*.html`, the nav key `budget`, `data-budget-head="spend"`, `.budget-head`); the old `/plan...` addresses no longer redirect (removed 2026-10-25). The JSON API stays at `/api/budget`, the assistants' tools keep their names (`get_budget`, `assign_to_category`...). **The Plan is the budget alone**: it once had Build (moving money into funds and paying down loans) and Earn (expected income) as extra chips, and they were taken out at the owner's request, because moving money between accounts does not change net worth (only income does) and the older envelopes already did the rest (migration `c4e6a8b0d2f4` dropped `accounts.saving`, `profiles.plan_build`, `plan_earn` and the plan rows of funds, loans and income accounts; nothing in the ledger changed). A fund or loan payment is budgeted as spending through the **category on its line** (nothing fills it in for the person; the account has no usual category since 2026-11-01). Savings goals were removed (see "Goals were removed" below). Below, "the budget page" means the Budgets page.

## The budget page's look

`/budget` has a "Budgets" title row (`.page-head.tall`, as tall as the rows of Accounts and Categories so the titles line up) with the month as a pill ("Oct 2026") at its right carrying both arrows (it can look ahead, unlike Categories), then **Left to spend** as one large figure with a full-width bar (`.ready-hero`, `.hero-top`) in `budget_body.html`, and **Ready to assign** as a quiet line under it (see "The head of Budgets" below); under it, in `.ready-warns`, one-line boxes: a **red** "Overspent by X" only when more was spent than there was to spend in all the categories together (`overspent` = minus the total `available` when negative; one overspent category is not a warning, its own bar shows it) and an **amber** "Over-assigned by X" when Ready to assign is negative (it then stays at ₹0.00; a plan that cannot be funded, nothing spent yet; `--warn`/`--warn-soft` tokens in all three blocks). A **group's page** still leads with the group's own left-to-spend figure and bar (`glance_block`, `_glance` in `routers/web/budget.py`: what was spent out of what there was to spend, with the tick for today). What needs a decision is **not on this page any more: it is under the bell** (see "The to-do bell" below); "Copy last month's amounts" is a quiet link under the head, only on a month with nothing assigned yet (any category row hides it; `copyable` is then zero) and where it would copy something; there is no "How this works" at the bottom any more (removed 2026-10-23: the ⓘ beside Ready to assign shows the same sum, and the "Everything else" row says what it is). The categories are a **ranked list, biggest budget first** (`_by_assigned`, `_hint`, the `row` macro in `partials/budget_rows.html`, `.crow`): one row per top-level category or group with its name, a hint line (a group's three biggest subcategories and "+N"; for a plain category what needs a look: "overspent last month"), what is left, "of <what there was>" and a thin bar with today's tick **that runs the full width of the row, under the name and the amount** (`.crow.has-bar` is a two-column grid, the bar spans both; 2026-10-21, a bar that stopped short of the row read as "partly full"; `test_every_bar_runs_the_full_width_of_its_row`). Nothing folds. Groups with nothing in them are tucked behind "N unused groups hidden · Show" once anything is in use (plain top-level categories always show). **A group opens its own page** (`GET /budget/group/{account_id}`, `budget_group.html`, `partials/budget_group_body.html`: a back link with the month pill beside it in one row (`.acct-nav`, the same pill and row as a category's page), then the group's name on a line of its own (`.group-title`, so a long name just wraps), the group's "Left to spend" figure and its categories ranked, with no row for the group's own money: the group's figure, bar and "Spent … of …" line are one tappable block (`glance_block`, `a.tappable`, a small chevron at the end of the last line; no pencil) that opens a sheet (`GET /budget/group/{account_id}/sheet`, `partials/budget_group_sheet.html`, `POST /budget/group/{account_id}/assign`) where the group's budget starts at what its subcategories add up to (`BudgetService.group_minimum`) and may be raised, the extra being kept on the group's own account in `budget_allocations` (`assign_group`: `total - minimum`), so a group's total still adds up its own money and its categories'; **below the minimum is refused**: the field turns red as it is typed (`data-min`, `aria-invalid`, the `groupMinimum` block in `app.js`), Save does nothing, and the server answers "Minimum X" (400) as well; what was posted straight to the group counts in its figure and bar); a plain category, and each row on a group's page, **opens a bottom sheet** (`GET /budget/category/{account_id}/sheet`, `partials/budget_sheet.html`, one `<dialog id="cat-sheet">` inside `#budget`) with the Assigned field (`POST /budget/assign` with `sheet=1`, errors in `#cat-sheet-error`; the response swaps `#budget`, which closes the sheet, and from a group's page the sheet carries `group=<category_id>` so the group page's block comes back instead of the main page's), what was spent and Move money. There are no inputs on the page. Only plain accounts that are not expense categories have no sheet (404). The row's `href` is the target page, the fallback without scripts.

## Budget (envelope budgeting)

`services/budget.py` (`BudgetService`) gives every unit of money a job, YNAB-style, **on top of the ledger without changing it**. The only stored things are the plan (`budget_allocations`: user, month = first day, **category**, amount; unique per month and category) and the flag `accounts.on_budget` (asset or liability accounts holding a currency; new asset accounts holding a currency default to yes, a card is opted in; the migration switched on existing asset leaves). Everything else is derived on demand, so editing the ledger can never leave it stale:

- *cash* = debits minus credits on the budget's accounts (a flagged credit card counts as money owed), up to the month's last day, **and for the current month only up to today** (`BudgetService._compute` caps `through`; 2026-11-02: what is dated later this month is not in cash or in any category's activity until its day, so the sum agrees with every account page, and Ready to assign barely moves because a payment out of the budget also leaves its category; other months are counted to their last day, and a later month counts what was dated ahead in the one before); flagged accounts with sub-accounts are left out (still listed as `ignored_accounts` in the API, no longer on the page);
- *activity* of a category = the lines that carry it (`postings.category_id`) in transactions that touch a budget account, where the line's own account is not part of the budget (an Expenses account's lines carry its category; a payment into a loan or fund carries the category it was labelled with); spending from outside the budget is ignored;
- *available* rolls month by month (`BudgetService._roll`): `max(0, last month's available) + assigned - activity`. Leftovers carry forward, but an overspent category (negative at the end of a month) starts the next month at **zero** and the deficit is taken out of *ready to assign* from then on (cash overspending, the YNAB way; credit-card purchases are treated the same). In its own month an overspent category just shows negative and ready to assign does not change yet. `overspent_last_month` reports what was deducted;
- *ready to assign* = cash minus the sum of all categories' available. Moving money between categories (`move`, limited to what is available) never changes it.

Budgeting **starts with the first month anything was assigned** (`start` in `_compute`): earlier spending is ignored, because cash is the balance now and past spending already left it (counting it as overspending would deduct it twice). Spending paid from an asset/liability account that is not part of the budget (typically a credit card never switched on) is not counted in a category; it is reported as `left_out` / `left_out_accounts` and the page offers "Add to budget" per account (`POST /budget/accounts/{account_id}/include`).

**Loans and other accounts outside the budget:** a payment into one (an EMI, a SIP) is a transfer whose line carries a category (`postings.category_id`): the budget counts it as spending in that category when the entry touches a budget account. The Add screen shows a Category line on such a row (optional, **left empty: nothing fills it in**; an account has no usual category since 2026-11-01, migration `c6e8a0b2d4f7` dropped `accounts.payment_category_id`; the budget flag changing no longer relabels anything). A payment saved with no category is not wrong, but no envelope took the money: `BudgetRead.uncategorised` lists them and the bell's To do sheet shows "N payments have no category" with a category to pick for each (`TransactionService.label_payment`, `POST /todo/payments/{transaction_id}/category`). Reports (`ReportService`): the breakdown and `summary.spent` are **by category** (the whole EMI is spending in its category); `summary.expenses` is the books' view (the Expenses account), where paying into a loan is no expense and only the interest the lender charges is.

**Setting up a month:** `BudgetService.copy_previous` (web "Copy last month's amounts", `POST /budget/copy`, MCP `copy_budget_from_previous_month`) assigns the month what each open category got the month before, only where nothing is assigned yet, in one write (`BudgetRepository.add_many`); `BudgetRead.copyable` is what it would copy. Money assigned to **later months** is subtracted from ready to assign in every month before them (`assigned_to_later_months`), so assigning ahead (by opening the next month and filling it in) uses up what is ready to assign now, and the same money reads the same from either month.

**Credit cards** (liability accounts that are part of the budget) are listed in a "Credit card payments" block above the categories: *to pay* is what is owed at the month's end (`BudgetCard.owed`, zero for a card in credit), with the month's *charged* and *paid*. Nothing needs assigning: it is the same arithmetic as before (a card's debt is subtracted from cash), now made visible, and `ready_to_assign = money_in_accounts - reserved_for_cards - available - assigned_to_later_months` always holds (`BudgetRead`; the page and `get_budget` show the sum).

Groups show the sum of their subcategories; a group posted to directly also gets an "(other)" row. Deleting a category deletes its plan rows. Web: `/budget?month=YYYY-MM` (inline assigning swaps `partials/budget_body.html`, `/budget/move` page), the account form has the "Part of the budget" checkbox, **on the new-account form too** (shown when the chosen parent is under Assets or Liabilities, an asset starting in and a card or loan outside; `data-budget-fields`, `budgetChoice` in `app.js`; hidden fields are disabled so they are not sent) (the quick-account sheet over the transaction form still takes the default); JSON API: `/api/budget/`, `PUT /api/budget/categories/{account_id}`; MCP: `get_budget`, `assign_to_category`, `move_budget_money`.

## Coming up (2026-11-02, `feature/coming-up`)

What is dated after today is not in the budget's figures until its day, so it has one list: `ComingUpService` (`services/coming_up.py`, `schemas/coming_up.py`) merges the **entries dated ahead** and the **occurrences recurring rules will record** (worked out from the rule with `schedule.first_after`, not stored; paused and ended rules leave) up to the end of this month (or `through`, at most 366 days ahead), keeping only what touches an account of the budget, soonest first; `total` is left out when money coming in and going out are mixed. A rule's occurrence and an entry look the same (a rule's row says "Repeats" and opens the rule). `GET /api/budget/coming-up` (ids and amounts, like the transaction API), the web page `GET /budget/coming-up` (`routers/web/coming_up.py`, `coming_up.html`; names are resolved in the router with the transactions page's `AccountIndex`) and `GET /budget/coming-up/note`, the one line ("2 payments coming up by 30 Nov · 1,570.00 ›") that the sum behind Ready to assign loads after the page (`calc_panel(..., coming=is_current)`, only on the current month's Spend chip; it says nothing when nothing is coming). The sum's note names its date ("counted up to today", "…the end of October"). An account's page says what its balance will be after the entries dated ahead (`balance_after`, the running balance of the last one, `[data-balance-after]`; occurrences of rules are not in it, as in the card above it). Tests: `tests/test_coming_up.py`, `tests/browser/test_coming_up.py`.

## The to-do bell (2026-10-30)

A bell in the top bar (a count beside it; in the sidebar on wide screens, "To do") opens a bottom sheet of **what needs a decision now**, worked out on the spot (`TodoService`, `app/services/todo.py`; `GET /api/todo/`; nothing is stored, so there is nothing to read or dismiss and an item goes when it is fixed): payments into an account outside the budget that no category took (one item, with a category to pick per payment), each account that pays for things but is not part of the budget this month (**Add to budget**; not for accounts under the built-in Loans and Investments groups, which are outside the budget by design) each running recurring rule that could not be recorded (**Open rule**) and **each card or loan bill due within 5 days or overdue and still unpaid** (`AccountService.bills_due`, `cards.DUE_SOON_DAYS`; "Visa bill is due in 2 days" / "overdue by 2 days", the amount and due date, **Pay bill** → the Add form for that account and amount; it goes when the bill is paid; no account is read while none has its days set). The web routes are `GET /todo/badge` (the count, swapped in out of band into `#todo-count-bar` and `#todo-count-side`; a hidden element in `base.html` fetches it **after the page has loaded**, so no page waits for it, and each redraw of the sheet asks again), `GET /todo/sheet`, `POST /todo/payments/{transaction_id}/category` and `POST /todo/accounts/{account_id}/include` (they answer with the redrawn sheet). The sheet's forms are inside `#todo-sheet-body`, which is in `SHEET_BODIES`: the skeleton placeholders show **only for a GET** that opens a sheet, never for a form inside it (that removed the form mid-request). Budgets lost its "Needs you" line, the overspending note (the category's own hint and the ⓘ sum say it) and the Copy button's panel. A new kind of to-do belongs in `TodoService`, `TodoRead` and the sheet partial. Tests: `tests/browser/test_todo_bell.py`, `tests/test_web_budget.py`, `tests/test_budget_categories.py`.

## Budget categories (2026-10-29)

The budget used to plan against Expenses accounts, so an EMI or a SIP needed a made-up account (`Expenses:EMI:Loans`) to hang a plan on. A category is now its own thing, a label on the lines of an entry, apart from the books' account names. Built on `feature/budget-categories` in eight phases; plan and decisions in the owner's artifacts.

- **Model (migrations `c7e9a1b3d5f8`, `e4a6c8d0f2b3`, `f5b7d9e1a3c4`).** `budget_categories` (`id`, name, `kind` **spending|earning**, `parent_id` for two levels (a category and its subcategories), `system_key` for the built-in Uncategorised, `closed_on`); `postings.category_id` (what the line was for), `budget_allocations.category_id` (the plan), recurring `category_id` (rule and rows). **Books are pure (2026-10-31, `feature/pure-books`):** a category has no account behind it. The ledger's other side of an expense or an income is the root `Expenses` or `Income` account itself (the only roots that take postings, `GROUP_POSTING_ROOT_NAMES`), and the line carries the category. Nothing can be made under Expenses or Income (`AccountService._validate_parent` refuses: "hold categories, not accounts"); `accounts.category_id`, the books-account feature (Settings › Books accounts) and `breakdown?by=account` were removed. The migration moved the lines of every former mirror account to the root (category kept), repointed recurring rules, deleted the mirror accounts and **refuses, changing nothing, if a non-mirror account exists under Expenses or Income**; it compares each category's monthly totals, every other balance and the both-sides count before and after, was rehearsed on a restored production dump (151 -> 61 accounts and back) and can be undone. Category ids equal the old account ids, so old category links keep working.
- **A line names an account, or only its category.** `PostingCreate.account` may be left out when the line names its `category`: `TransactionService._fill_accounts` posts it to Expenses (a spending category) or Income (an earning one). A **category id where an account id goes is refused** (404 "account … not found"; it was accepted for a day after the migration, to keep old clients working, and removed on `feature/remove-bridges`). The same for recurring rows (`RecurringPostingData.account` optional, `RecurringService._rows_on_roots`); a simple rule pays a category with `to_account` = the Expenses/Income account plus `category`. The transactions `account` filter takes accounts only; `category` filters by label. The web forms still pick an expense or income by its category in the row's select; `split_postings(..., places)` (`category_places`) turns that choice into the Expenses/Income account plus the category before the service sees it. A line on Expenses or Income with no category becomes Uncategorised (`TransactionRepository._line_categories`, `CategoryRepository.ensure_uncategorised`); a category must fit the line (`_check_categories`: spending on Expenses or a payment out of the budget, earning on Income, nothing on Equity). An entry may not have the **same account and, on Expenses/Income, the same category** on both sides. `CategoryService` (`services/category.py`) is standalone: create, update (rename, move, note), close, reopen, delete-or-close (an unused category is removed with its plan, one with lines, subcategories or a rule is closed), merge, `register`; API `/api/categories/` incl. `GET /{category_id}/register`; the web pages are `routers/web/categories.py` (`/categories`, `/new`, `/quick`, `/{category_id}`, `/edit`, `/delete`, `/reopen`) with `category.html`, `category_form.html`, `partials/quick_category.html`, `partials/category_entries.html`. The Add form's pickers get `PickOption`s (`posting_groups`, `category_groups` in `routers/web/accounts.py`; optgroups "Expenses" and "Income" for the categories, so the JS is unchanged); lists name a line on Expenses/Income by its category (`AccountIndex.line_name`, `AccountService._counter_name`); the trial balance and the assistants write it as the journal would (`Expenses:Food:Groceries`). **Assistants:** `mcp_server/accounts.py` keeps accounts and categories apart: `AccountIndex` finds accounts only (and names categories for display, `where`), `CategoryIndex` finds categories, and `Places.find` turns the text of a from/to place or a row into an account or a category (`Place`), refusing a name that fits both or several; the money-moving tools build lines with `_sides`, `_postings`.
- **Files.** Backup **version 5** has a `categories` list (lines and rules carry the category; plan rows `category_id`, read from `account_id` in older files); an older file's Expenses/Income accounts become categories on restore (`BackupService._upgrade`). The ledger export writes a category as `Expenses:Food:Groceries` (declared and used); the import turns any path below Expenses or Income into a category (`new_categories` in the summary) and a `; category: EMI` label on a loan line into a spending category; the CSV has a `category` column. Deleted-for-good categories are not restorable (a category with history is closed and restored with Restore).
- **Required or not.** Expense and income entries always have a category (the account supplies it). A transfer needs none unless money leaves the budget into an account outside it, where it is optional with the "no category" nudge. Imports, the API and assistants use the accounts' categories or `CategoryService.uncategorised` (made on demand; found by `system_key`, not name).
- **Where it shows.** Add and recurring forms (`syncRowCategories`, `syncRowPickers` in `app.js`: the Category row of a payment into a loan; the same bottom sheet instead of a select for every row, with the select kept hidden underneath so the form logic is unchanged; do not rewrite the button's text between a press and its release, Safari drops the click), Categories page, the to-do bell.
- **The category is a chip (2026-10-29).** On the Add and recurring forms the category side of an expense or income is **a chip after the accounts, with its amount beside it** (`.txn-side.cat-side`, `onCategories` in `recomputeTxn`; `applyKind` puts the category section after the account one and hides the swap button, which a category and an account do not share): the chip (`.row-pick.pill`, a tag glyph; **an account is the same pill with a bank glyph**, 3rem tall and as wide as its column, "Account…" / "Category…" when empty) is the first row of the From/To logic, so one category holds the whole amount in grey and **typing less splits it naturally** (a row for the remainder appears, "left to allocate" speaks); removing down to one row gives that category the whole amount again. There is no "Split across categories" link (built and removed the next day at the owner's request: splitting must happen as you type). A payment into a loan or fund shows its Category as the same pill (hidden together with its select) with a "No category" choice. **The category sheet is a grid of tiles** (`.filter-options.grid`, two columns, the chosen one inverted, a small group name under a tile or row outside its group's heading (search), and a chosen account or category reads as its own name on the chip, never as "Food › Dining"), the account sheet a list with balances; "how much is left this month" under each tile was mocked and not built (it needs the budget on every Add page).
- **Files and API.** The backup (version 4 and later) carries each line's, rule's and row's category (a file before maps every line to its account's category) and `category_key` for Uncategorised; the ledger export writes `; category: EMI` on a line whose label its account does not say, the import reads it back, the CSV has a `category` column. `/api/categories/` (list, create, get, patch, close, reopen, delete, merge), `category` on posting lines and recurring rules; assistants have `list_categories`, `create_category` (write level) and `update_category`, `close_category`, `reopen_category`, `merge_categories` (manage level), and a `category` parameter where a line or rule is written. `category_id` is the audit's parameter name for a category id.
