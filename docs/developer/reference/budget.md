# The budget

Envelope budgeting on top of the ledger, the Budgets page, categories, Coming up and the to-do bell. Dates in headings are when a decision was made. Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-07; the rules summary is in `AGENTS.md`.

## The Budgets page (was the Plan, and the Budget page before)

**The Budgets page (2026-10-21; Build and Earn removed 2026-10-22; renamed 2026-10-23).** The screen, the tab and the sidebar say **Budgets** (no switcher since goals went, 2026-11-05); its address is `/budget` (`features/budget`, `/api/views/budget`); the old `/plan...` addresses are gone (2026-10-25). The JSON API stays at `/api/budget`, the assistants' tools keep their names (`get_budget`, `assign_to_category`...). **The Plan is the budget alone**: it once had Build (moving money into funds and paying down loans) and Earn (expected income) as extra chips, and they were taken out at the owner's request, because moving money between accounts does not change net worth (only income does) and the older envelopes already did the rest (migration `c4e6a8b0d2f4` dropped `accounts.saving`, `profiles.plan_build`, `plan_earn` and the plan rows of funds, loans and income accounts; nothing in the ledger changed). A fund or loan payment is budgeted as spending through the **category on its line** (nothing fills it in for the person; the account has no usual category since 2026-11-01). Savings goals were removed (see "Goals were removed" below). Below, "the budget page" means the Budgets screen.

## The budget page's look

`/budget` (`BudgetPage`, `budget_view.py`) has a "Budgets" title row with the month pill at its right (it can look ahead, unlike Categories), then **Left to spend** as one large figure with a full-width bar, and **Ready to assign** as a quiet line under it (see "The head of Budgets" below); under it one-line warnings: a **red** "Overspent by X" only when more was spent than there was to spend in all the categories together (`overspent` = minus the total `available` when negative; one overspent category is not a warning, its own bar shows it) and an **amber** "Over-assigned by X" when Ready to assign is negative (it then stays at ₹0.00; `--warn`/`--warn-soft`). What needs a decision is **not on this screen: it is under the bell** (see "The to-do bell" below); "Copy last month's amounts" is a quiet link under the head, only on a month with nothing assigned yet and where it would copy something (`copyable`). The categories are a **ranked list, biggest budget first** (`_by_assigned`, `_hint`): one row per top-level category or group with its name, a hint line (a group's three biggest subcategories and "+N"; for a plain category what needs a look: "overspent last month"), what is left, "of <what there was>" and a thin bar **that runs the full width of the row** (2026-10-21: a bar that stopped short read as "partly full"). Nothing folds. Groups with nothing in them are tucked behind "N unused groups hidden · Show" once anything is in use (plain top-level categories always show). **A group opens its own screen** (`/budget/groups/{category_id}`, `GroupPage`, `/api/views/budget/groups/{category_id}`): the month pill beside the way back, the group's name, its "Left to spend" figure and bar with the pace notch, and its categories ranked. The group's figure opens a sheet where the group's budget starts at what its subcategories add up to (`BudgetService.group_minimum`) and may be raised, the extra kept on the group itself (`assign_group`: `total - minimum`); **below the minimum is refused**: the field says so as it is typed and the server answers "Minimum X" (400). A plain category, and each row on a group's screen, **opens the category's sheet** (`CategorySheet`): the Assigned amount (`PUT /api/budget/categories/{category_id}`), what was spent and Move money.

## Budget (envelope budgeting)

`services/budget.py` (`BudgetService`) gives every unit of money a job, YNAB-style, **on top of the ledger without changing it**. The only stored things are the plan (`budget_allocations`: user, month = first day, **category**, amount; unique per month and category) and the flag `accounts.on_budget` (asset or liability accounts holding a currency; new asset accounts holding a currency default to yes, a card is opted in; the migration switched on existing asset leaves). Everything else is derived on demand, so editing the ledger can never leave it stale:

- *cash* = debits minus credits on the budget's accounts (a flagged credit card counts as money owed), up to the month's last day, **and for the current month only up to today** (`BudgetService._compute` caps `through`; 2026-11-02: what is dated later this month is not in cash or in any category's activity until its day, so the sum agrees with every account page, and Ready to assign barely moves because a payment out of the budget also leaves its category; other months are counted to their last day, and a later month counts what was dated ahead in the one before); flagged accounts with sub-accounts are left out (still listed as `ignored_accounts` in the API, no longer on the page);
- *activity* of a category = the lines that carry it (`postings.category_id`) in transactions that touch a budget account, where the line's own account is not part of the budget (an Expenses account's lines carry its category; a payment into a loan or fund carries the category it was labelled with); spending from outside the budget is ignored;
- *available* rolls month by month (`BudgetService._roll`): `max(0, last month's available) + assigned - activity`. Leftovers carry forward, but an overspent category (negative at the end of a month) starts the next month at **zero** and the deficit is taken out of *ready to assign* from then on (cash overspending, the YNAB way; credit-card purchases are treated the same). In its own month an overspent category just shows negative and ready to assign does not change yet. `overspent_last_month` reports what was deducted;
- *ready to assign* = cash minus the sum of all categories' available. Moving money between categories (`move`, limited to what is available) never changes it.

Budgeting **starts with the first month anything was assigned** (`start` in `_compute`): earlier spending is ignored, because cash is the balance now and past spending already left it (counting it as overspending would deduct it twice). Spending paid from an asset/liability account that is not part of the budget (typically a credit card never switched on) is not counted in a category; it is reported as `left_out` / `left_out_accounts` and the page offers "Add to budget" per account (`POST /budget/accounts/{account_id}/include`).

**Loans and other accounts outside the budget:** a payment into one (an EMI, a SIP) is a transfer whose line carries a category (`postings.category_id`): the budget counts it as spending in that category when the entry touches a budget account. The Add screen shows a Category line on such a row (optional, **left empty: nothing fills it in**; an account has no usual category since 2026-11-01, migration `c6e8a0b2d4f7` dropped `accounts.payment_category_id`; the budget flag changing no longer relabels anything). A payment saved with no category is not wrong, but no envelope took the money: `BudgetRead.uncategorised` lists them and the bell's To do sheet shows "N payments have no category" with a category to pick for each (`TransactionService.label_payment`, `POST /todo/payments/{transaction_id}/category`). Reports (`ReportService`): the breakdown and `summary.spent` are **by category** (the whole EMI is spending in its category); `summary.expenses` is the books' view (the Expenses account), where paying into a loan is no expense and only the interest the lender charges is.

**Setting up a month:** `BudgetService.copy_previous` (web "Copy last month's amounts", `POST /budget/copy`, MCP `copy_budget_from_previous_month`) assigns the month what each open category got the month before, only where nothing is assigned yet, in one write (`BudgetRepository.add_many`); `BudgetRead.copyable` is what it would copy. Money assigned to **later months** is subtracted from ready to assign in every month before them (`assigned_to_later_months`), so assigning ahead (by opening the next month and filling it in) uses up what is ready to assign now, and the same money reads the same from either month.

**Credit cards** (liability accounts that are part of the budget) are listed in a "Credit card payments" block above the categories: *to pay* is what is owed at the month's end (`BudgetCard.owed`, zero for a card in credit), with the month's *charged* and *paid*. Nothing needs assigning: it is the same arithmetic as before (a card's debt is subtracted from cash), now made visible, and `ready_to_assign = money_in_accounts - reserved_for_cards - available - assigned_to_later_months` always holds (`BudgetRead`; the page and `get_budget` show the sum).

Groups show the sum of their subcategories; a group posted to directly also gets an "(other)" row. Deleting a category deletes its plan rows. The account form has the "Part of the budget" switch, **on the new-account form too** (by kind: an asset starts in, a card or loan outside); the quick-account sheet over the Add screen takes the default. JSON API: `/api/budget/`, `PUT /api/budget/categories/{category_id}`, the views `/api/views/budget`, `/api/views/budget/groups/{category_id}` and `/api/views/budget-move`; MCP: `get_budget`, `assign_to_category`, `move_budget_money`.

## Gentler first steps (2026-10-21)

For a new person, without taking anything from the others: (1) **Home's "Get started" steps** (`features/home/GetStarted.tsx`, from `services/home.py`) are three: add an account, record your first spend, give your money a job (any plan row); they show on an empty Home and, **while steps are left**, under the figure after the first entry. (2) **The Budgets welcome** (`show_intro`: nothing ever assigned) disappears with the first amount. (3) **The account form asks one budget question**: a single "Part of the budget" switch, set by where the account goes (it was folded under "Budget options" with a payment-category choice until 2026-11-01). Starter categories were considered and left out (freeze).

**Over the plan (2026-10-21):** a category past its budget is red (`.crow .bar.over`, a minus figure, the "Overspent by" box, Home's overspent line). Nothing in the arithmetic changed (`tests/test_web_plan_heads.py`).

**Budgets' head is Ready to assign again (2026-10-23).** The one large figure is **Ready to assign** (`data-plan-head="budgets" data-ready-to-assign`, never below zero) with its faint ⓘ and the sum behind it, and a quiet note: "Give it a job: tap a category", "Every ₹ has a job" at zero, "Nothing to assign yet" with nothing planned, or an amber `.hero-warn` "Over-assigned by X · take some back from a category" (the old amber box is now this line on Budgets). **Left to spend and its bar are only on Home** (and a group's own page); `_head` and the quiet `.rta-line` are gone. The identity and every figure are unchanged. The red "Overspent by X" box stays under the head.

## Coming up (2026-11-02, `feature/coming-up`)

What is dated after today is not in the budget's figures until its day, so it has one list: `ComingUpService` (`services/coming_up.py`, `schemas/coming_up.py`) merges the **entries dated ahead** and the **occurrences recurring rules will record** (worked out from the rule with `schedule.first_after`, not stored; paused and ended rules leave) up to the end of this month (or `through`, at most 366 days ahead), keeping only what touches an account of the budget, soonest first; `total` is left out when money coming in and going out are mixed. A rule's occurrence and an entry look the same (a rule's row says "Repeats" and opens the rule). `GET /api/budget/coming-up` (ids and amounts, like the transaction API) and `GET /api/views/coming-up` (the Coming up screen, `services/coming_up_view.py`, names resolved with `AccountIndex`). The sum behind Ready to assign on the current month carries one line, "2 payments coming up by 30 Nov ›", read after the screen and opening Coming up; it says nothing when nothing is coming. The sum's note names its date ("counted up to today", "…the end of October"). An account's page says what its balance will be after the entries dated ahead (`balance_after`, the running balance of the last one; occurrences of rules are not in it, as in the card above it). Tests: `backend/tests/budget/test_coming_up.py`, `backend/tests/api/test_api_small_views.py`, `backend/tests/e2e/test_small_pages.py`.

## The to-do bell (2026-10-30)

A bell in the top bar (a count beside it; in the sidebar on wide screens, "To do") opens a bottom sheet of **what needs a decision now**, worked out on the spot (`TodoService`, `backend/finode/services/todo.py`; `GET /api/todo/`; nothing is stored, so there is nothing to read or dismiss and an item goes when it is fixed): payments into an account outside the budget that no category took (one item, with a category to pick per payment), each account that pays for things but is not part of the budget this month (**Add to budget**; not for accounts under the built-in Loans and Investments groups, which are outside the budget by design) each running recurring rule that could not be recorded (**Open rule**) and **each card or loan bill due within 5 days or overdue and still unpaid** (`AccountService.bills_due`, `cards.DUE_SOON_DAYS`; "Visa bill is due in 2 days" / "overdue by 2 days", the amount and due date, **Pay bill** → the Add form for that account and amount; it goes when the bill is paid; no account is read while none has its days set). The app reads `/api/views/todo` after the screen has loaded, so nothing waits for it, and reads it again after each fix; a payment's category is set with `POST /api/transactions/{transaction_id}/category` and an account joins the budget with `POST /api/budget/accounts/{account_id}/include`. Budgets lost its "Needs you" line, the overspending note (the category's own hint and the ⓘ sum say it) and the Copy button's panel. A new kind of to-do belongs in `TodoService`, `TodoRead` and `features/todo`. Tests: `backend/tests/e2e/test_small_pages.py`, `backend/tests/api/test_api_small_views.py`, `backend/tests/budget/test_budget_categories.py`.

## Budget categories (2026-10-29)

The budget used to plan against Expenses accounts, so an EMI or a SIP needed a made-up account (`Expenses:EMI:Loans`) to hang a plan on. A category is now its own thing, a label on the lines of an entry, apart from the books' account names. Built on `feature/budget-categories` in eight phases; plan and decisions in the owner's artifacts.

- **Model (migrations `c7e9a1b3d5f8`, `e4a6c8d0f2b3`, `f5b7d9e1a3c4`).** `budget_categories` (`id`, name, `kind` **spending|earning**, `parent_id` for two levels (a category and its subcategories), `system_key` for the built-in Uncategorised, `closed_on`); `postings.category_id` (what the line was for), `budget_allocations.category_id` (the plan), recurring `category_id` (rule and rows). **Books are pure (2026-10-31, `feature/pure-books`):** a category has no account behind it. The ledger's other side of an expense or an income is the root `Expenses` or `Income` account itself (the only roots that take postings, `GROUP_POSTING_ROOT_NAMES`), and the line carries the category. Nothing can be made under Expenses or Income (`AccountService._validate_parent` refuses: "hold categories, not accounts"); `accounts.category_id`, the books-account feature (Settings › Books accounts) and `breakdown?by=account` were removed. The migration moved the lines of every former mirror account to the root (category kept), repointed recurring rules, deleted the mirror accounts and **refuses, changing nothing, if a non-mirror account exists under Expenses or Income**; it compares each category's monthly totals, every other balance and the both-sides count before and after, was rehearsed on a restored production dump (151 -> 61 accounts and back) and can be undone. Category ids equal the old account ids, so old category links keep working.
- **A line names an account, or only its category.** `PostingCreate.account` may be left out when the line names its `category`: `TransactionService._fill_accounts` posts it to Expenses (a spending category) or Income (an earning one). A **category id where an account id goes is refused** (404 "account … not found"; it was accepted for a day after the migration, to keep old clients working, and removed on `feature/remove-bridges`). The same for recurring rows (`RecurringPostingData.account` optional, `RecurringService._rows_on_roots`); a simple rule pays a category with `to_account` = the Expenses/Income account plus `category`. The transactions `account` filter takes accounts only; `category` filters by label. The Add screen picks an expense or income by its category in the row (`EntryRow.choice`); `EntryService.postings` (`category_places`) turns that choice into the Expenses/Income account plus the category before the transaction service sees it. A line on Expenses or Income with no category becomes Uncategorised (`TransactionRepository._line_categories`, `CategoryRepository.ensure_uncategorised`); a category must fit the line (`_check_categories`: spending on Expenses or a payment out of the budget, earning on Income, nothing on Equity). An entry may not have the **same account and, on Expenses/Income, the same category** on both sides. `CategoryService` (`services/category.py`) is standalone: create, update (rename, move, note), close, reopen, delete-or-close (an unused category is removed with its plan, one with lines, subcategories or a rule is closed), merge, `register`; API `/api/categories/` incl. `GET /{category_id}/register`; the app's screens are `features/categories` (`/api/views/categories…`, `/api/views/category-form`). The Add screen's pickers get `PickOption`s (`posting_groups`, `category_groups` in `services/pickers.py`; groups "Expenses" and "Income" for the categories); lists name a line on Expenses/Income by its category (`AccountIndex.line_name`, `AccountService._counter_name`); the trial balance and the assistants write it as the journal would (`Expenses:Food:Groceries`). **Assistants:** `mcp_server/accounts.py` keeps accounts and categories apart: `AccountIndex` finds accounts only (and names categories for display, `where`), `CategoryIndex` finds categories, and `Places.find` turns the text of a from/to place or a row into an account or a category (`Place`), refusing a name that fits both or several; the money-moving tools build lines with `_sides`, `_postings`.
- **Files.** Backup **version 5** has a `categories` list (lines and rules carry the category; plan rows `category_id`, read from `account_id` in older files); an older file's Expenses/Income accounts become categories on restore (`BackupService._upgrade`). The ledger export writes a category as `Expenses:Food:Groceries` (declared and used); the import turns any path below Expenses or Income into a category (`new_categories` in the summary) and a `; category: EMI` label on a loan line into a spending category; the CSV has a `category` column. Deleted-for-good categories are not restorable (a category with history is closed and restored with Restore).
- **Required or not.** Expense and income entries always have a category (the account supplies it). A transfer needs none unless money leaves the budget into an account outside it, where it is optional with the "no category" nudge. Imports, the API and assistants use the accounts' categories or `CategoryService.uncategorised` (made on demand; found by `system_key`, not name).
- **Where it shows.** The Add and recurring screens (the category line of a payment into an account outside the budget, picked in our own sheet), the Categories screen, the to-do bell.
- **The category is a chip (2026-10-29).** On the Add and recurring forms the category side of an expense or income is **a chip after the accounts, with its amount beside it** (`.txn-side.cat-side`, `onCategories` in `recomputeTxn`; `applyKind` puts the category section after the account one and hides the swap button, which a category and an account do not share): the chip (`.row-pick.pill`, a tag glyph; **an account is the same pill with a bank glyph**, 3rem tall and as wide as its column, "Account…" / "Category…" when empty) is the first row of the From/To logic, so one category holds the whole amount in grey and **typing less splits it naturally** (a row for the remainder appears, "left to allocate" speaks); removing down to one row gives that category the whole amount again. There is no "Split across categories" link (built and removed the next day at the owner's request: splitting must happen as you type). A payment into a loan or fund shows its Category as the same pill (hidden together with its select) with a "No category" choice. **The category sheet is a grid of tiles** (`.filter-options.grid`, two columns, the chosen one inverted, a small group name under a tile or row outside its group's heading (search), and a chosen account or category reads as its own name on the chip, never as "Food › Dining"), the account sheet a list with balances; "how much is left this month" under each tile was mocked and not built (it needs the budget on every Add page).
- **Files and API.** The backup (version 4 and later) carries each line's, rule's and row's category (a file before maps every line to its account's category) and `category_key` for Uncategorised; the ledger export writes `; category: EMI` on a line whose label its account does not say, the import reads it back, the CSV has a `category` column. `/api/categories/` (list, create, get, patch, close, reopen, delete, merge), `category` on posting lines and recurring rules; assistants have `list_categories`, `create_category` (write level) and `update_category`, `close_category`, `reopen_category`, `merge_categories` (manage level), and a `category` parameter where a line or rule is written. `category_id` is the audit's parameter name for a category id.
