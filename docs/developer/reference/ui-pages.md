# The web UI: page by page

What each page shows and how it behaves: Home, Add, Transactions, Accounts, Categories, Settings and smaller pages. Dates in headings are when a decision was made. Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-07; the rules summary is in `AGENTS.md`.

## The Add screen's polish (2026-10-24)

The amount fields of the transaction form (`data-group`: the total and each row's amount) are **grouped as they are typed** (`groupAmount` in `app.js`: `1,24,500` for rupees; the cursor stays after the same digits; `tidyField` groups too, and the form groups what the page was opened with); the server strips commas (`parse_amount`), so values are sent grouped. `--n` (the width of the big amount) counts a comma as about half a digit. **A side with one row has no ×** (`recomputeTxn` and the load handler hide it; `.txn-row:has([data-remove-row][hidden])` gives the name its width back); a split shows it on every row. **The date is a chip** ("Today, 24 Oct", `templating.date_chip`, `label.date-chip` over an invisible `input[type=date]`; `app.js` renames it on change and opens the picker on click) and the note is a plain field with the placeholder "Note (optional)": no labels above either. 

## Home polish (2026-10-24)

"View all ›" is quiet (`.quiet-link`), the day label (`.tx-day-head`) lines up with the heading and the card edge, the gap between the bar's caption and "Transactions" is 1.5rem, and **from 60rem the page column is centred in the space beside the sidebar** (`body.app main` margin-left: `50vw + sidebar/2 - 22rem`; the same on every page). **A row that touches the system's Opening Balances** (an account's opening balance or a balance adjustment) names only the other account in its second line, and the stock note "Initial account balance" is left out (`transaction_days.html`; a note of the person's own still shows).

## One row for Budgets, Accounts and Categories (2026-10-24)

The three lists share one row: **a name, one quiet second line, the figure at the right** (Budgets adds the bar under it). Budgets' second line is "of 25,000.00", then "all spent" when nothing is left or a red "overspent" when it is (2026-11-04; the figure and the bar carry the amount: "over by 1,900.50" was cut off on a narrow phone and repeated the figure), then what needs a look (`partials/budget_rows.html`; **the rows carry no pace notch any more**, it stays on Home and in a group's own head). Categories' second line is the biggest subcategories and the share ("Dining · 13%"), or "43% of spending" ("of earnings" on Income), and "Nothing yet" with a quiet "–" when the month has nothing (`categories.html`). Accounts rows carry **a small dot after the name when the account is part of the budget** (`.budget-dot`; the line under the figure says "in the budget" once, `.budget-key`, only when one is shown; 2026-11-03). Accounts rows have **no round initials** and the group headings (name and subtotal) line up with the row text and amounts (`.acc-gh`).

## Transactions polish (2026-10-24)

The details sheet's title leads like the row (the payee, else where the money went: never "No payee"); Repeat, Related and Delete are one line of words under Edit (`.sheet-links`; Delete is red). The Filter sheet's row names are dark and the choices grey, as in Settings. A row that touches the system's Opening Balances (`tx-system`: opening balances and adjustments) has a muted title and figure, in every list.

## Profile and settings

There is **one page for the person and the application: Settings** (`/settings`, the avatar in the top bar and the sidebar's link; 2026-10-23). It has a title row like the other pages, then tiles in groups (2026-10-24): **the profile row** (the name or "Your profile", the email as its value; it **opens the name form in place**, `details#name.setting`, `POST /profile`, flash `profile-updated`), **Preferences** (currency, appearance), **Your data** (backup/export/import, recurring transactions, AI assistants) and **About** (feedback, **About this test** `details#about`, and **Sign out** as a red row). A row's value sits right beside its chevron (`.setting-value + .icon`). `/profile/data` and `/profile/assistants` use the same tiles (a small gap between, headings close to their text, Erase and Delete as red rows). **Both are rows that open in place (`details.setting`, 2026-11-04):** Your data has Export, Backup, Import and, under "Erase or leave", the two red rows (ids `export`, `backup`, `import`, `erase`, `delete-account`); AI assistants has the Connected tile, then Sign in with Claude, Use a token and Setup instructions, and the token form's access level is one segmented choice (`.scope-seg`, radios named `scope`) with a sentence under the chosen one (CSS only; the consent page keeps its three cards). `/profile` has no page of its own (only `POST /profile` saves the name); the sub-pages have a "Settings" back link. Put every new setting on the Settings page (`routers/web/settings.py`, `settings.html`; forms post to `/settings/...`). Storage is unchanged, described next.

`app/models/profile.py` holds one `profiles` row per user (names, currency) (keyed by the Supabase user id, created lazily by `ProfileRepository.get_or_create`). Per-user details and application settings are **typed columns on that table** — add a setting with a column, a migration and a field on `ProfileUpdate`, not a key-value or JSON store. Supabase `user_metadata` is deliberately not used: users can write it directly and it is copied into every token. `/settings` has collapsible `<details class="setting">` rows (currency, appearance) that `app.js` opens when the URL hash names them; `/profile/data` is Your data (export, backup, import, erase). The light/dark override is per browser, kept in the `finode_theme` cookie that `base.html` reads, not a profile column. The JSON API is `/api/profile/`. Email and password stay with Supabase.

## Home, quick add and payee chips

**Home** (`routers/web/dashboard.py`, `dashboard.html`) is one number: "Left to spend in <month>" from the budget (`BudgetService.view`: `available`, with "Spent X of Y" and a thin bar; "Over budget" in red when negative), or, until something is assigned or carried in (`glance.has_budget`: spending with no plan is not "over budget"), the month's spending with a link to set up the budget; then, once something is recorded, the transactions directly (the overspent warning line and the collapsible credit row were removed 2026-10-24 to be revisited; mock-ups A–E are in the owner's artifacts; the budget still computes `cards` and `overspent`) as as plain rows under a "Transactions" heading: the latest entries for this month, **the chosen month's own entries, newest first, when the month pill is on an older month** (up to `RECENT_LIMIT`; "View all" opens `/transactions?month=`; an empty month says so). With nothing recorded yet it shows "Nothing recorded yet" and the getting-started steps. Net worth left Home (`ReportService.summary` still computes it). New transactions start from the usual floating "+" (phones) or the sidebar button; there is no chat-style bar on Home (it was tried and dropped as unclear to new people).

**The figure and the bar** (`dashboard.py`, `dashboard.html`, `.glance`, `.box-top`, `.home-strip`): no box (a bento box around it was built, deployed and dropped the same day at the owner's request, 2026-10-20). The label ("Left to spend" or "Over budget", with the "i") and **the month pill at the top right** share the top row (`?month=YYYY-MM`, never a month ahead, `max` = this month; a past month shows its own figures, bar without the pace tick, Earned/Spent from `reports.summary` for that month); then the figure and the bar. Under the bar one quiet caption says what it shows (**"Spent so far X of Y"**, `.glance-caption`, "Spent" for a past month; 2026-10-24); there is no "Details" word and (the Earned / Assigned / Spent columns were removed 2026-10-23 as too busy; the Summary page they once linked to was removed at the owner's request, 2026-10-20, with `ReportService.month_report`). Balance, Owed and the comparison with last month are gone. Transactions follow the bar directly (no overspent line any more).

**Payee chips** (`suggested_payees` in `routers/web/transactions.py`, `GET /transactions/suggest?payee=`, `partials/payee_chips.html`, `usePayee` in `app.js`; **names only since 2026-11-01**: the owner did not want accounts or categories chosen for them): under the payee field on the Add screen, **only while something is typed** (an empty field shows none, `suggested_payees` answers `[]`; the chips are not loaded with the page, and the row goes once one is chosen; 2026-11-02): the people you pay most that match (`TransactionService.payees()`, most used first, six at most; an exact match first, then starts-with, then contains). **They are one row on any width**: chips that would wrap are hidden (`fitPayeeChips` in `app.js`, class `fit-hidden`, rerun on resize), and the field's placeholder reads "Payee or Merchant?" (2026-10-24). Tapping a chip fills the payee, removes the row and moves focus to the amount (or Save when it has one); nothing else is filled in, and typing a known payee in full does nothing either. A new form starts with **no account chosen** and no category (the account you paid from most lately was pre-filled until 2026-10-24; the owner did not want it); opening it from an account's or category's page, or Repeat, is the person's own doing and still fills what it says. Chips use `data-suggest` and `data-payee` attributes, not `data-kind`, which belongs to the Expense/Income/Transfer buttons. Edit forms show no chips.

## The transactions list, search and the details sheet

`/transactions` has a title row with the **month pill** at the right (the same place as Budget and Categories: `.page-head.tall`; `?month=YYYY-MM`, default this month, never a month ahead; the list is that month, the current one also listing what is dated ahead in the upcoming card; `date_from`/`date_to` in a link still win), then the count line and the Filter button (see "The Transactions filter" below). **There is no search box** (dropped at the owner's request, 2026-10-20; `q` still works in a link and `TransactionRepository.list(text=...)` is unchanged: it matches the payee, the note or the name of any account used, case-insensitive, `%` and `_` taken literally). `?kind=expense|income|transfer` still works in a link (`KIND_FILTERS`, `kind_accounts`; told apart as the rows are: a debit to Expenses is spending, else a credit to Income is income, else a transfer). **Rows open a bottom sheet**, on an account's or category's own page too (their rows and the upcoming card carry `?back=<that page>`, which the sheet passes on to Edit, Repeat and Related and to the sheet of a related entry, so you return where you were; `account.html` holds its own `<dialog id="txn-sheet">`) (`GET /transactions/{transaction_id}/sheet`, `partials/txn_sheet.html`, one `<dialog id="txn-sheet">` per page inside `partials/transaction_list.html`, so Home's recent rows open it too): payee, date, amount, the accounts (labelled by kind: Paid from/Spent on, From/Received in, From/To; every row of a split), the note (a row only when there is one), the related entries (**since 2026-10-26 related entries, the source and the assistant/recurring line sit behind one "More" row, `details.sheet-extra`, left-aligned like the rows above it, label only, with a chevron at the right; it keeps a 24px+ gap above Edit and the sheet grows upwards so Edit never moves under a finger**), **a quiet line above the buttons only for an assistant (`Added by <name>`, the `robot` glyph, blue `--auto`) or a recurring rule (`Recurring`, the `repeat` glyph, links to /recurring); nothing for web, API or import entries (2026-10-24; `.sheet-by`)**, and **Edit**, **Repeat** (a a text link in the line under Edit with Related and Delete (2026-10-24); `/transactions/new?repeat=<transaction_id>`: a copy of the entry's rows, payee and note, dated today) and **Delete** (confirmed; the error shows in `#sheet-error`, which is why the delete route takes `sheet=1`). Rows keep `href` to the edit page as the fallback when scripts do not run (htmx `hx-get`, opened by `htmx:afterSwap` in `app.js`). **Entries dated ahead are not history**: Home's Transactions list (the latest entries) lists only entries dated today or earlier (`transactions.list(date_to=local_today())`), and `/transactions` lists them in **one card** at the top: closed, one line "N upcoming · X in all" (the total of what they come to; left out when an income entry is among them); opened, a statement (`details.upcoming`, `.up-row`: the date down the left, payee and route, amount; soonest first, first page only, up to `UPCOMING_LIMIT`; a search or filter opens it only when no history matches, so a match is never hidden; with history to show it stays closed), while the main list and its paging are history only. An **account's page** does the same (`account_context`: the "Transactions" list is what has happened; what is dated ahead sits in the same one card at the top of that list, under its heading, a row opening the entry's edit page and back; the total is left out when money in and out are mixed); the card is one shared macro (`partials/upcoming.html`, fed `UpcomingItem`s from `routers/web/utils.py`). A category page lists only what has happened in the month, so it has no such card. **An account's page shows only its newest entries**: `AccountService.register_page` gives `REGISTER_PAGE` (30) entries up to today, finished to the end of their day so a day is never split, plus everything dated ahead; the running balance comes from the account's postings alone (`TransactionRepository.posting_facts`: no payees or other accounts) and only the shown entries have their transactions and counter-accounts read. **Load more** (`.more-entries`, htmx `GET /accounts/{account_id}/more?offset=N`, `partials/account_entries.html`) replaces itself with the next chunk. `AccountService.register` (all entries, the JSON API and a category's month) is unchanged.

## The month pill

Budget, a group's budget page, Categories and a category's page share one macro (`partials/month_pill.html`, `month_pill(url, month_name, month_key, previous, next, max)`; `url` holds `{month}`): **‹ Oct 2026 ›**, the arrows (`.mp-arrow`, one month back or forward, each a 2.5 rem tap target at either end so they cannot be mixed up) around the month, which is a **native month picker** (`input[type=month].month-input` laid invisibly over its name, `.month-pick`; `monthPicker` handlers at the end of `app.js` go to `data-url` with the chosen month and call `showPicker()` on desktop). Pages that never look ahead pass `max` (`this_month` from `_month_context`): the picker refuses later months and the forward arrow is shown faded, not a link. Native on purpose (iOS and Android wheels); a browser without a month picker shows a text box, so only a complete YYYY-MM inside min/max is taken. Browser test in `tests/browser/test_categories_page.py`.

## Accounts and Categories

The money is split over two pages: **Accounts** (`/accounts`) lists Assets, Liabilities and Equity (`ACCOUNT_ROOTS`), **Categories** (`/categories`, `routers/web/categories.py`) lists the budget categories (its chips read **Spending · Earning**, `?tab=expense|income` and `?tab=spending|earning` both work). A category is not an account (see "Budget categories"): `POST /categories` creates one, `GET /categories/new` is its own form, and `/accounts/{id}` of a category is a 404.

`/accounts`: see "The accounts page" in [ui-look.md](ui-look.md) (chips, the total, the type's groups and accounts). An account's (or category's) page opens with a **title row** (the name with a small "i" beside it when the account has a description, and the type under it as one small line; on the right one round "…" menu (`details.menu`) whose first row is Edit (2026-11-04: the pencil left); the description opens from the "i" in the same place as the menu and each closes the other (`details.note-info`, a handler at the end of `app.js`), so it takes no room on a page that is visited every day), then the balance (no Add button: the docked "+" follows the page; `.acct-title`); a category adds the month as the same pill as Categories and Budget (`.cat-bar`), on the left above the figure, with no label (the figure is the month's total up to today, or the whole month for a past one).

`/categories?month=YYYY-MM&tab=expense|income`: one month (never a future one; the current month counts up to today), a heading of three rows (the title with the month pill at its right, as on Budget (`.page-head.tall`); one row with the full-width Expense and Income switcher, `.cat-bar`; the total as one large figure with no visible label: the chips say which it is, and a screen-reader-only label ("Spent so far", "Spent in <month>", "Earned…") keeps it readable), and a **ranked list**: one row per top-level category, biggest first (`categories_page`, `_figures`, the `row` macro, `.crow`), with its name, a hint line (its three biggest subcategories that month and "+N"), what it came to and its share of the month (`<1%` under one percent, `0%` for one with nothing in the month, so every row is the same two lines tall, the name on the first line); nothing folds. **There is no chart and no colour here** (a coloured share bar and dots were tried and removed at the owner's request, 2026-10-19; the `--chart-*` tokens went with them), on a category's page either. Tapping a row opens the category's page, which lists its **subcategories** biggest first (a "Subcategories" tile, `subs` in `category_page`) and "Everything else" for what was posted to the category itself, then the month's transactions **posted to this category alone** (`register(category_id, own_only=True)`; each subcategory's entries are on its own page, while the figure above stays the whole category's month). The dashed **"+ Add category" row at the end of the page** (`.add-row`, the same row as "+ New recurring"; the empty page keeps its "Add a category" button) opens `/categories/new?root=<tab>`, whose Under list holds only that kind (Expenses or Income, `CATEGORY_TABS`), starting on it, as the Accounts "+" does for its type. **Every open top-level category is listed, with nothing in the month too** (they follow the biggest ones at 0.00 and 0%; the "Load more" that tucked them away was removed 2026-10-23; only deleted and closed categories are left out); the page has no hint line under the list, the rows say they can be tapped. Amounts come from `ReportService.account_totals` (one pass over the month's postings) and are rolled up in the router. `/categories/{account_id}?month=` is the account page template with `category=True`: the back link and the month pill share one row above the title (`.acct-nav`; the type stays under the title), then the month's total instead of the balance; there is **no Add button**: the Subcategories heading (`.section-head`) carries a round + (`.circ-button`, as on the Categories page) that adds one inside this category (`/categories/new?parent=`; only where `can_add_child`, and the heading is not shown at all while a category has none, so the first subcategory comes from the … menu's "Add subcategory"); the Transactions heading has no + (2026-11-04: the docked "+" already adds an entry for the category). **An account with sub-accounts** (a group such as Assets › Banks) cannot be posted to, so its page has no Transactions list: a "Sub-accounts" tile lists them (name and balance, each opening its own page, closed ones left out) with a round + that adds one (`account_context` skips the register for it); every transaction is on the sub-account's own page. The word is **subcategory**, one word, everywhere; the page also has that month's transactions, "See in the Budget" in the menu; Close/Reopen stay, Pin does not apply to categories. Budget stays the place to plan; Categories is what happened.

**The Add screen's rows have no pill and no amount field.** A side lists its accounts (or categories) as slim rows (`.row-pick.slim`: glyph, name, `.rp-when` "Posts 18 Oct" only when the line's date differs from the entry's, `.rp-amt` only when its amount differs from the total, or "450.00 left" on the empty row), and one "Choose account…" row while something is left to allocate (the same open-row logic as before: `recomputeTxn`, `data-touched`, suggestions). Each `[data-row]` still holds a hidden `select[name=account]`, a hidden `input[name=amount]` (typed nowhere on the row), a hidden `input[name=posted_on]` and a never-shown remove button, so the kind buttons, swap, allocation and the form post are unchanged. **Tapping a row opens the picker sheet** (`#row-picker`), which only picks: one tap on an option closes it and sets the select (the amount and date are no longer in it; 2026-11-02). **A chosen row has a settings glyph at its right** (`.row-settings-open[data-row-settings]`, sliders, made by `ensureGear` in `syncRowPickers`, hidden while the row is empty, `.set` when the row's amount or date differs) that opens a small sheet, `<dialog id="row-settings">` (in `partials/txn_rows.html`; the block "The settings sheet of a row" in `app.js`): the account's name, then (a native `input[type=date]`, `min` the entry's date, only on an account side of the entry form, `data-posting-dates`), AMOUNT and DATE side by side, a full-width **Done** pill (`.row-done[data-config-ok]`) and a red **Remove** below it (`[data-config-remove]`, which clicks the row's hidden remove button); both fields are pre-filled (the row's amount, its posting day or the entry's date); Done sets the amount (touched only when it changed) and `posted_on` (empty when it is the entry's date) and dispatches `change` on the select. A category row has the glyph with the amount only. A payment's optional Category (`[data-category-select]`) keeps its own pill and picks at once. The same rows serve the recurring form (no date there). `tests/browser/conftest.py` has `row_button`, `row_gear`, `set_row`, `add_row` for the browser tests.

**The Transactions filter replaces the kind chips (2026-10-23).** The Expense · Income · Transfer switcher and "More filters" are gone. Under the title row a line says "N transactions" (a plain count of the month's history; with a filter set and every match going one way, "N transactions · ₹X spent|earned" when a filter is set and every match goes one way: `_summary`) with one **Filter** button at its right (`.tx-filterbar`, `[data-open-filter]`, "Filter · N" when set). It opens `<dialog id="tx-filter">` (`transactions.html`, a bottom sheet): three rows, **Account** (`account`, any non-category account and its sub-accounts), **Category** (`category`, an Expenses or Income account; the repository's `also_ids`, so both must be touched) and **Merchant** (`merchant`, an exact payee, capitals aside, `TransactionRepository.list(payee=...)`; the choices are `payees()`, most used first, without "Opening balance" and "Balance adjustment"), each opening a searchable list in the same sheet (`app.js`, the `#tx-filter` block; the lists are rendered with the page by `_filter_options`). What is set shows under the line as plain text with a ✕ (`.tx-applied`, `_applied`). With a filter set the page reads the whole match for the month (`everything`) to count and total it, then pages from that. The month pill still scopes everything. `?kind=` and `?q=` keep working in links (no control for them); tests that look for text absent from the list must cut the sheet out (it names every account and payee).

**Show more instead of pages (2026-10-23).** Home and the Transactions page load the chosen month in chunks, in place: the first 8 (Home) or 25 (Transactions) and then `GET /transactions/more` (`more_transactions`; `offset`, `size`, `scope=home|list`, the month and every filter travel in the address `_more` builds), which answers the next days and the next row (`partials/transaction_more.html`, `transaction_days.html` shared with the list, `transaction_more_row.html`; `.more-row` is "Show N more · M left", at the end "That is all of October · N transactions"). **A day is never split between two chunks** (`TransactionService.page`: the chunk runs on to the end of its last day, counts only when something is left). Home lists the month's own entries (this month up to today), no longer the latest ones of any month; the "Newer/Older" pager and `?page=` are gone. It needs scripts (htmx), like every sheet. `/transactions/more` is a LISTS route in the isolation audit; the query ceilings of `/transactions` and `/` were raised (10 and 15) for the count and the day that runs over.

**Recurring and Tags pages are calm lists (2026-10-27).** `/recurring` (title "Recurring", back link Settings) is the shared row: the payee (else where it goes), one quiet line ("Every month · next 1 Nov", "· paused", "· ended", or the red "Couldn't record: …") and the amount; paused and ended rules sit under a "Paused" heading, muted; the route and end date live on the edit page; a dashed `.add-row` "New recurring" ends the page and the empty state is one sentence. `/tags` has no intro: each row is the blue `#tag` and its count (`.tag-name`, `.tag-count`); a tag's sheet has the uses and last day, a blue "View entries" row and "Rename" and "Merge into…" as folded rows (`details.sheet-extra` with the form inside), then Delete.

## The Add screen's headings (2026-11-04)

Both tiles carry a heading in the words of the details sheet, which follow the kind (`KINDS` in `app.js`, `applyKind`): Expense **Paid from / Spent on**, Income **From / Received in**, Transfer and "other" **From / To**. The category tile's heading is no longer hidden and the hint beside each heading ("the account you paid with"...) is gone. Two tiles always, so nothing changes shape when a side is split.

## Two removals (2026-11-05)

The recurring form's hint about a past start date shows only while the start date is in the past (`[data-past-hint]`, toggled by `app.js` with the start chip), and the empty state of Coming up no longer carries a Back button (the page has its back link).

## One way to add (2026-11-05)

A group's page ends its Sub-accounts tile, and a category's page its Subcategories tile, with the dashed `.add-row` ("+ Add sub-account", "+ Add subcategory"), like Accounts, Categories and Recurring; the round "+" in a heading is gone everywhere. Sub-account rows carry the budget dot like the Accounts page.

## Sign-in without a card (2026-11-05)

Sign in, sign up, forgot and reset (`body.bare`) have no card: the form is centred on the page (`body.bare main` is a flex box and `.card.narrow` has `margin: auto`, so a long sign-up still scrolls) under a larger wordmark. `tests/browser/test_sign_in_page.py`.

## First run (2026-11-05)

Home's steps (`partials/get_started.html`, `ol.steps.tile`) are rows you can tap (a chevron at the right, the next step's number filled in `.step-mark.now`, finished ones ticked), under "Get started · N of 3", with one line of small print (the import). Budgets with no account in it says one sentence and has one button, "Choose accounts" (`/accounts`); its intro box shows only once an account is part of the budget. `tests/test_web_budget.py`.

## A group's page and its Transactions (2026-11-05)

A category with subcategories shows a "Transactions" heading only when something was posted to the category itself; otherwise the page ends after "+ Add subcategory" (its entries are on the subcategories' own pages, and "Nothing in November." beside a figure that is not zero read as a contradiction).

## Move money picks in the sheet (2026-11-05)

`/budget/move` has no native menus: From and To are `.field-pick` buttons over hidden `select[data-sheet-select]` that open `#row-picker` in its flat mode, each category showing what it has left ("300.00 left", `data-balance`); the same attribute and button serve the Under field of the account and category forms (`data-noun` names what is picked). The selects are not browser-required, so `POST /budget/move` answers an empty From or To with a message in `#form-error`.

## Opening balances fold (2026-11-05)

On a day with two or more "Opening balance" entries (a new ledger adding its accounts) the lists (`partials/transaction_days.html`: Home, Transactions, Show more) show one quiet row, "Opening balances · N accounts" (`li.tx-fold`, a `details`), that opens to the entries, each still a row with its own sheet. One opening balance stays a normal row, a balance adjustment is never folded, and an account's own page (`account_entries.html`) is unchanged. `tests/test_opening_fold.py`.
