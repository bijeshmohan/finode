# Accounts and categories

## The idea

Money is always *somewhere* and always *for something*. finode keeps both as accounts in one tree:

- **Accounts** are where money is: Assets (bank, cash, wallet, investments) and Liabilities (credit cards, loans). Equity holds the system's *Opening Balances* and is folded away.
- **Categories** are what money is for: Expenses (Food, Rent) and Income (Salary).

Every transaction moves an amount *from* one place *to* another, and the books stay balanced because both sides are recorded. You never have to think about debits and credits to use it.

## Accounts page

**Accounts** lists Assets and Liabilities with their totals. Each group (for example *Bank*) has a heading with its subtotal and its accounts under it; plain accounts share one tile. Things to know:

- **Recently used** cards at the top show the last five accounts you used by hand; swipe them.
- The **search box** filters by name or path.
- Accounts you never use are tucked behind *N unused accounts hidden · Show*.
- Closed accounts sit behind *N closed accounts · Show*.
- Accounts go **two levels** deep, at most: a group and the accounts in it.

### Add an account

Tap the round **+** on the Accounts page, give the account a name, pick where it goes (Assets or Liabilities, or inside a group) and enter today's balance. The balance becomes an *Opening balance* entry against the system's Opening Balances account.

An account page shows its balance, an **Add transaction** button, a pencil for Edit, a **…** menu, and its transactions (the newest 30, then **Load more**). Entries dated in the future are in one **upcoming** card at the top of that list.

### Colours and signs on an account's page

- **Green with a `+`** is money that raises an asset account, or a **payment into a card or loan** (it lowers what you owe).
- A **charge on a card** is plain, with no sign; the running balance beside each row shows which way it went.

### Credit cards and loans

A card or loan is a **liability**: its balance is what you owe. Paying the bill is a *transfer* from your bank account to the card. To have the budget count a card's purchases as spending, turn on **Part of the budget** in its edit page. A loan outside the budget can name a **payment category**, so an EMI counts as spending in that category.

### Closing and deleting

An account that holds a balance cannot be closed. A closed account keeps its history but disappears from pickers; **Reopen** it any time. Closing is under **Advanced** (see [Settings](recurring-and-settings.md)). An unused account can be deleted for good. Both can be brought back from **Recently deleted** (the bin icon on the Accounts page).

## Categories page

**Categories** shows one month at a time (never a future one). Pick **Expense** or **Income**. The big figure is the month's total (*Spent so far* during the current month). Below it is a ranked list, biggest first, each row with its three biggest sub-categories and its share of the month. Tap a row to see the category's page: its sub-categories and the month's transactions. Use the arrows on the month pill to move between months.

To add one, tap the round **+**. A category can have sub-categories one level down (Food › Groceries), no further.

**Deleting a category:** if it has never been used it is removed. If it has history it is *closed* instead: it leaves your lists and pickers but stays in the months where it has amounts. **Restore category** brings it back. Both are under the **…** menu and in **Recently deleted** (the bin icon).

## Notes

Names can be up to 40 characters and cannot contain a colon (`:`). A category can carry a short note, shown on the Budget.
