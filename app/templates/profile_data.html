Full backup

One file with everything: settings, your own assets and prices, accounts (including which are part of the budget, and closed ones), transactions, recurring transactions and your budget. Keep it somewhere safe. It does not include access tokens or connected apps, which you set up again.

{% if can_import %}

Start from an existing ledger or hledger file. You see a summary first, and nothing is saved if anything is wrong. Your account depth limits apply to the accounts in the file.

{% else %}

Importing is only available before you record transactions. Export your data first if you want to start over.

{% endif %}

Erase or leave

These can't be undone. Download a full backup first (above): it is the only way back.

Erase my books

Deletes your accounts, transactions, budget, recurring transactions, own assets and prices, and the change history. You keep your login, your name and settings, and your assistants' access, so you can start again from an empty ledger.

Delete my account {% if can_delete_login %}

Erases everything above and also removes your login, your settings and every assistant token and connected app. You are signed out for good, and you would have to sign up again to come back.

{% else %}

Deleting your login isn't set up here. Erase your books above and ask the person who runs finode to remove your login.
